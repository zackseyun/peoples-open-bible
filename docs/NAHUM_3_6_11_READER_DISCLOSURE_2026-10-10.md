# Nahum 3 6 and 3 11 reader disclosure

The proposed reader notes disclose two published Hebrew alternatives without
selecting a new source or changing POB's marker-free main English. They also
correct three misplaced note anchors. This makes real source uncertainty
available to readers while preserving the unresolved priority decision in the
[Nahum source comparison](NAHUM_PESHER_AND_GREEK_FOLLOWUP_2026-10-10.md).
It is not a fresh decipherment or a claim that the current translation is optimal.

## Meaning and note placement

At3:6 the existing sight/spectacle note is attached to “treat you with contempt”
instead of “a spectacle.” Its marker moves to the latter phrase. The expanded
note retains the literal sight gloss and discloses the approximately “make you
repulsive” reading in the biblical quotation at4Q169 fragments3–4, iii2.
The filth note and its anchor remain unchanged.

At3:11 the refuge/stronghold note is attached to drunkenness, and the
drunkenness note is attached to hiddenness. Their markers move to “refuge” and
“become drunk,” respectively; their original note text and reasons are preserved.
A new note at the end of the refuge clause discloses4Q169 fragments3–4, iv7's
city expression. The opening verb there is supplied, and unpointed spelling
does not decide “in a city” against “in the city.”

Both disclosures leave inherited quotation wording and adaptation for the
commentary possible. Mur.88's decisive3:6 ending and3:11 refuge/enemy expression are
supplied, not preserved support for the shorter Masoretic forms. Its3:11
seeking verb survives; the supply limit concerns the words after that verb. The notes
do not name a specific city or a city-of-refuge institution.
