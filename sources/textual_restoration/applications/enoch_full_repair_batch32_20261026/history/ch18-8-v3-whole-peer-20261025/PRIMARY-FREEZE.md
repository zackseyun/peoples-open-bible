## Selected printed source, thirteen words

1. ወማእከላይሰ — wa + ma'kalāy + sa. Middle/central one, with attached distinguishing/contrastive particle; NOT 'their middle' and NOT a possessive -homu. Actual col785 adjective entry has medius, specifically de spatio and Hen18,8. The final arch is first-order Sat, as in the first letter of word4 and word13, not a normalized sixth-order dictionary-letter substitute. The preceding two letters are la (IV) and y (VI), not an imagined nominal -lo.
2. ይጐድእ — third masculine singular imperfect, with g/u-containing glyph. Actual col1203 I,1 beta is attingere, followed by eske, with Hen18,8, distinct from alpha quate(re) and from the head's more general percutere/pulsare. Here 'reaches/touches', not 'strikes'.
3. *እስከ — eske, endpoint 'unto/as far as'. Asterisk is editorial marking, not a fidel or a spoken word. Actual col750 B temporal/spatial preposition section includes a termino usque ad, usque in, tenus.
4. ሰማይ — heaven, exact first-order Sat and sixth-order Yaman. Actual col341 caelum. Superscript45 is apparatus; not a word, suffix, or translation content.
5. ከመ — comparison 'like/as', not causal or reported-speech use. Actual col826 A,I,1a comparative and col827 B prepositional instar/tamquam/quasi.
6. መንበረ — throne/seat in dependent construction before la-Lord. Actual col654 sense1 sedes/scamnum/solium/thronus, not the separate status/condition noun in col655.
7. ለእግዚአብሔር — la + Lord (God), dependent possession/association of throne; actual compound col1192 dominus terrae vel regionum i.e. universi, naming God. Not an extra first-person/third-person suffix.
8. ዘእምእብነ — za relative + em material/source + stone in dependent form; 'which is of stone'. Full col758 stone entry continuing759, lapis, not peccatum entry preceding it in757. Restricts throne phrase, not an independent new subject silently supplied by a story.
9. ፔሐ — stone-name; col1392 actual entry album marmor, fortasse alabastrites explicitly cites Hen18,8. 'White marble' is supported; 'alabaster' alone is more specific than the entry's tentative alternative. Superscript46 is apparatus.
10. ወደማሑ — wa + top/summit with third-singular masculine suffix. Col1085 under II,1 vertex: b cujusvis rei ut montis, throni Hen6,6;18,8;89,29. The suffix can anticipate the dependent throne phrase; it is not independent evidence for a new unnamed object.

12. እምእብነ — em + stone, material 'of stone'; superscript48 apparatus. Not from among a set of stones as an English narrative inference.
13. ሰንፔር — first-order Sat, N sixth, P fifth, R sixth. Actual col375 sapphire entry (two orthographic forms), sapphirus and Hen18,8. Not normalized to the first neighboring lemma or a Greek spelling. Superscript49 apparatus; following full stop ends unit before9.
