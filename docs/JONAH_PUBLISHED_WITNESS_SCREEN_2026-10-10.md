# Jonah published Hebrew witness screen

The four Hebrew records in the existing Jonah discovery map contain several
published differences worth adjudicating, but this screen does not establish a
replacement for POB's source text. The clearest candidates concern Jonah2:7,
3:2 and4:6. All48 current POB source and main-English verse records were
inspected; none is changed here. References follow POB's Hebrew numbering,
including the fish narrative at2:1 rather than English1:17.

## Controls and unresolved leads

4Q76,5:18 substitutes בשלמי for WLC's באשר למי at1:8, but POB already conveys
“on whose account.” Its extra conjunction at1:9 likewise does not require a
new English conjunction. The4Q81 screen found no surviving difference requiring
a changed English rendering; supplied causal wording is not independent
support against4Q76. At1:7,4Q76,5:17 and4Q81,1:4 have ויפילו against
WLC's ויפלו, a verb-form difference with the same “cast lots” English sense.
Shared storm, question and vow wording provides positive
comparison controls. Spelling differences are not promoted merely because they
produce a different consonant string.

Three additional4Q82 leads remain weaker: the printed2:8 transition lacks the
prayer-and-temple expression, but damaged and supplied context prevents a
secure deletion claim without checking the edition's reconstruction and space;
2:9 has uncertain ח֯ס֯ידם֯ with parenthetic ח֯ס֯ודם֯, not an established new
“faithful people” translation;4:7's apparent added היום is only partly
preserved as [הי]ו֯ם. These are follow-up questions, not secure complete-word
readings or approved changes. Fragment and line continuity must not be inferred
from adjacent biblical verse labels alone.

## Next evidence and stopping rule

For each of the three stronger candidates, consult the passage's critical
apparatus and original publication conventions, identify the relevant hands
and local transmission relationships, and compare versional wording without
assuming a unique Hebrew back-translation. A defensible earlier-form argument
must explain both readings; an English proposal must then explain its syntax
and meaning separately. Photograph review is useful if a decisive published
letter is contested, not a mandatory prerequisite to using published texts.

Do not repeat this screen or ask models to vote on the same extracts. Resume a
candidate when a specific apparatus unit, editorial discussion, or discriminating
image becomes available. This work bears on wording, not the authority or canon
status of Jonah or another book. Source attestation alone cannot decide which
books a religious community should regard as canonical.
