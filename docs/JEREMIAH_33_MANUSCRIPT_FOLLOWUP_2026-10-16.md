# Jeremiah 33: manuscript margin and restoration boundaries

## Result for POB

The actual Marchalianus page corroborates the distinction reported in the
[first comparison](JEREMIAH_33_COVENANT_COMPARISON_2026-10-16.md): its main Greek
text goes from the material of Hebrew 33:13 to 34:1, while a smaller marginal
block transmits 33:14–26. The physical 4Q72 plate also resolves the earlier
major supply-boundary concern. Neither result changes POB's Hebrew, English,
reader notes or canon. Priority between the long and short literary forms
remains unresolved; no newly recovered letter is accepted.

This follow-up **supersedes the first report's statement that DJD and QDR have
different major supply endpoints awaiting reconciliation**. That frozen report
and its evidence packet remain unchanged as the record of the earlier judgment.
The [new evidence packet](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/jeremiah33_manuscript_followup.2026-10-16.v1.json)
records this correction, image bindings and limits. This is a context-aware
manuscript/edition comparison, not blinded transcription or spectral recovery.

## Marchalianus: the target is page 484

Root reacquired the Vatican's actual IIIF manifest for Vat.gr.2125 and navigated
the images by their text. Pages 475, 480, 482 and 483 were navigation controls,
not evidence that the target marginal block had already been found. Page 483
ends within the towns/flocks unit. The target is the page labelled **484**,
canvas **p0975**, and its paired higher-resolution capture **p0976**. These are
two photographs of the same manuscript page, not two witnesses. The custodian's
[viewer](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Vat.gr.2125/0976) and
[manifest](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiif/MSS_Vat.gr.2125/manifest.json) identify the
object; image identifiers are not modern verse numbers.

Root viewed both complete target captures and deterministic top/left crops.
A delegated source reader independently checked the target with its proposed
identification disclosed. The visually secure layout and content controls are:

| Region | Observation | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large main writing, opening lines | Continuation of the towns/flocks unit: Jerusalem environs and Judah, counting flocks and the Lord's formula, followed directly by the word to Jeremiah concerning Nebuchadnezzar's siege | Actual short junction, Greek 40:13 → 41:1 / Hebrew 33:13 → 34:1 |
| Smaller top block | “Behold, days are coming,” the good word, David's righteous branch, Judah/Jerusalem, David's throne, priests/Levites and offerings, then the day/night covenant opening | Positive Greek transmission of the long block, physically separate from the main line |
| Smaller left continuation | Day/night covenant, Davidic and priestly promises, the rejected families, heavenly/earthly ordering, Jacob/Abraham/Isaac and restoration/mercy ending | Continuation through the material of 33:26, not an isolated short gloss |
| Start of smaller top block | A theta-shaped attribution label and asterisk signs are visible | Corroborates the location and presentation behind Swete's θ′/asterisk/Qmg report, without independently dating the hand |

These are phrase anchors and an extent/layout check, **not a newly certified
letter-by-letter diplomatic transcription**. Contractions, exact spellings,
all critical signs and individual attribution changes have not been collated.
The separate darker cursive annotations are not flattened into the smaller
majuscule supplement. No claim that every marginal item has one author or date
follows from their location or ink contrast.

Swete's own previously consulted key remains the basis for interpreting its
printed θ′ as Theodotion and Qmg as Marchalianus margin. A theta-shaped mark
alone does not recover the translator's autograph or date this copy's marginal
hand. The modern target apparatus and specialist hand/attribution assessment
remain necessary before making those stronger claims. This Greek supplement
is not a second direct Hebrew manuscript or proof that the original Greek
translation contained the block.

The Vatican permits personal study but requires permission for publication or
reproduction of its images. Captures and crops remain private; this Git record
publishes locators, hashes and observations, not the archival pixels.

## 4Q72: the major brackets agree when read right-to-left

The source reader located and checked **DJD XV, Plate XXXVI / PDF page 380**,
top-left fragment **55 ii / column XXV**. Root also viewed the complete plate,
fragment detail and printed transcription-boundary crop. The caption binds
fragments 51, 53 and 55 to **Mus. Inv. 245, PAM 43.101**. Its separate
“computerized image” caption concerns fragments 47–48, not 55.

Read in Hebrew order, DJD's brackets and the QDR primary rows have the same
major boundaries:

| Line | Published surviving run or boundary | Still supplied or uncertain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Through `יהוה צד` | `קנו` and the David/no-cutoff clause supplied |
| 2 | Through `איש יושב על כסא בי` | Remaining house/Israel wording and Levitical/no-cutoff clause supplied |
| 3 | Through `מלפני מעלה עלה ומקטיר` | Following grain-offering/sacrifice/continuous-time clause supplied |
| 4 | Through `ויהי דבר יהוה אל ירמיהו` | Following speech and covenant opening supplied |
| 5 | Covenant/night wording with partial surviving runs; boundary after the following `ל` | Opening supplied; QDR's uncertain final yod of `בריתי` retained; he of `הלילה` and following continuation supplied |

The previous apparent disagreement arose from interpreting the bidirectional
display's square brackets as left-to-right range markers. This is a correction
to our reading of the edition, not a new scroll reading or a change to either
edition. The plate shows the narrow surviving fragment, not the absent long
continuations. It supports the physical limits but cannot independently grade
each tiny torn-edge stroke: folds, blur and surface darkness remain material
limits. QDR's circlet is not promoted to a certain letter. No new geometric
reconstruction or missing word is inferred.

Thus the ancient Hebrew inclusion evidence remains positive but partial.
Especially, the reconstructed Davidic/Levitical clauses must not be advertised
as completely preserved ink merely because the long Hebrew form is otherwise
well known. The tentative copy date and unknown composition date remain as
qualified in the first dossier.

## Modern apparatus: rejected false lead, not a completed consultation

A bounded delegated search tested three new lawful routes and stopped. The
publisher preview whose search/OCR layer suggested Jeremiah actually renders
as **Hanhart, Esdrae liber II, second edition**. Root visually checked its title
and publisher list. It is excluded from Jeremiah textual evidence. Its actual
list names Jeremiah XV, fourth edition 2013, whereas the consulted Logos record
identifies 2006; whether this reflects revision or reprinting is not established
here. No changed apparatus reading is inferred from an edition number.

The [publisher preview](https://www.vandenhoeck-ruprecht-verlage.com/downloads/productPreviewFiles/LP_978-3-525-53400-7.pdf)
was privately acquired despite a separate web-reader denial. The actual
[Logos Jeremiah product](https://www.logos.com/product/180087/jeremias-baruch-threni-epistula-jeremiae)
provided metadata/purchase access, not consulted target pages. The official
Academy download for Ziegler's 1958 supporting study returned 403 by both tested
routes. No access restriction was bypassed and no purchase was attempted.
None of these is a modern Jeremiah target-apparatus consultation.

## What would change the judgment next?

Obtain the actual Göttingen XV target text and supplementary/reviser apparatus
for 40:13 → 41:1, with its own key and the consulted edition identified. Use
it to test the supplement's attribution, hands and wider witness distribution,
not simply count main text and margin as opposing independent manuscripts.
The source-priority question then needs a discriminating transmission argument;
the older physical Hebrew copy does not itself date the block's composition.

Do not repeat the now-completed target-location or major-bracket checks as new
evidence. Better 4Q72 captures would be relevant only to a specific disputed
trace. More tokens or same-image enlargement do not create missing material.
This pass improves evidence accuracy and closes two concrete gaps, without
claiming a translation-changing discovery or grounds for enlarging the canon.
