## Request text

Please provide access to the original wavelength-by-wavelength image files
and available capture/calibration metadata for Paris, BnF, Coislin 202,
folio 1v (Codex H / GA 015). The project's digital edition presents this
physical leaf as folio 2v (direct text, 1 Corinthians 10:27-29) and folio 3r
(ink transfer, 1 Corinthians 10:29-32). A subset for this one physical leaf
would suffice; the complete approximately 241GB collection is unnecessary.

The proposed research uses non-generative alignment, exposure normalization
and spectral contrast to assess surviving traces near the two lines marked
illegible after the verse 32 material in the current 3r XML. Original images
will be preserved, and editorial completions will remain distinct from observed
writing. The public processed views have not yielded a new accepted reading
in our inspection.

Please also identify any existing publication, edition note or supplementary
reading addressing those two lines, and any known physical or imaging limit
that makes further recovery unlikely. We would welcome the correct file-to-leaf
mapping and processing information for the published direct and ink-transfer
views. Please confirm the citation and reuse conditions applicable to the
provided files; the dataset record currently specifies CC BY-NC 4.0.

Thank you for making these research materials available.
