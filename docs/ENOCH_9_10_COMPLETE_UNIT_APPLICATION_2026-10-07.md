# Complete selected Enoch9–10 units — bounded draft application

The resumed full-book Graphic Reader goal authorizes source repair and release.
This application reviews all33selected units (9:1–11and10:1–22), replaces25
existing complete/clipped records and creates8missing units. It does not
certify the entire book, a manuscript collation or a human-specialist review.

Selected evidence is Charles1906GeEz critical-edition recovery checked against
printed18–30/PDF56,58,60,62,64,66,68, including cross-page unit endings and
apparatus. The original source critical marks remain. The only source cleanup
is9:11’s detached ASCII18: the scan places that note at the following Xheader,
not inside IX11. Original parser/model recovery remains preserved separately.
No full oracle text or modern copyrighted English version is imported.

Important editorial decisions:
-9:3’s bracketed address remains disclosed as an interpolation in the selected
edition.9:4’s corrupt “to kings, because” fragment is preserved in its note,
not silently converted to a singular King or imported Lord-of-the-ages title.
-9:6’s corrupt heavenly-secrets clause is not silently rewritten as “kept.”
9:11keeps the doubtful them-clause and direct address to God, not an invented
third-party messenger. Semyaza’s spelling is consistent with this sequence.
-10:1keeps the corrupt selected messenger-name transliteration;10:2discloses
the parenthesized Noah command as Charles’s editorial supply with reported
Greek support, not independent Ethiopic manuscript attestation.
-10:7marks “disclosed” as an English contextual conjecture for a corrupt verb,
retaining the daggered original source.10:11keeps make-known/announce rather
than silently importing bind; the binding command is explicit in10:12.
-10:14keeps an uncertain burning reading, not an unsupported condemned verb.
10:15retains the conjunction rather than certifying group identity.
-10:19corrects the model’s thousand to ten thousand for the printed myriad
word. The olive/oil measures remain uncertain; this is textual abundance,
not a scientifically validated yield forecast or literal illustration count.

The orthographic corroboration for the myriad term is the GeEz.org manuscript
character proposal, PDF123, which names እልፍ as ten thousand:
https://emufi.geez.org/docs/UnencodedManuscriptEthiopic.pdf
