# 1 Samuel 13:1 — inherited numbers versus conjecture

Checked 2026-09-06. Retain the declared WLC source and main English
provisionally; clarify the evidence categories in the reader note. This is not
a recovery of Saul's original regnal figures or a comprehensive apparatus review.

## Evidence and decision

The local `sources/ot/wlc/1Sam.xml`, `1Sam.13.1`, has `בן שנה` and `ושתי שנים`,
matching the canonical verse. No qere or supplied larger number is encoded at
this locus. WLC and its POB copy are linked representations, not two witnesses.
The existing English renders the accession idiom and the transmitted two-year
figure; translating them does not certify their historical accuracy.

[NET's textual notes 1–2](https://classic.net.bible.org/verse.php?book=1Sa&chapter=13&verse=1)
report thirty as an age reading in some Greek copies, while most omit the verse.
They distinguish the proposed forty-two-year reign as a combination of Samuel's
two and Acts' forty. This directly consulted editorial explanation is not a
fresh Greek-manuscript collation. POB's SBLGNT Acts 13:21 independently confirms
what the local Acts source says: `ἔτη τεσσεράκοντα`, forty years. It is a separate
narrative source, not a Hebrew manuscript of Samuel.

The present Samuel note groups witnesses and later proposals without identifying
which number has which support. The correction separates them. The alternative
of supplying larger numbers makes the narrative easier to understand but does
not identify recoverable Hebrew wording. Conversely, retention is not proof
that the inherited numbers are original. We do not silently change the accession
idiom into “Saul had reigned one year” to remove the difficulty.
