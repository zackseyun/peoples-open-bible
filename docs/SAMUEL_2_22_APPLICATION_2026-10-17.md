# 1 Samuel 2:22 — provisional source and English application

## Decision and meaning

The exact reviewed shorter source and its English have now been applied to
the repository's [1 Samuel 2:22](../translation/ot/1_samuel/002/022.yaml).
This is a consequential, provisional editorial contribution to POB, not a
newly discovered manuscript reading or a recovery of the autograph.

> ועלי זקן מאד וישמע את אשר עושים בניו לבני ישראל׃
>
> Eli was very old, and he heard what his sons were doing to the people of Israel.

The main text no longer includes the explicit allegation about lying with
women at the sanctuary. The full longer Masoretic allegation remains in the
verse's textual note, alongside the contrary evidence and uncertainty. This
does not establish that the reported conduct did not occur. Choosing the
earliest recoverable wording and judging historical events are different tasks.

The [complete source assessment](SAMUEL_2_22_FULL_SOURCE_AND_ENGLISH_2026-10-17.md)
remains the scholarly basis. Support is feature-specific: the participial
ending and recipient expression have stronger preserved-letter support than
the first omitted כל. The shorter ending partly depends on published spatial
reconstruction. Positive longer Greek reception and Josephus, and the
possibility of shared omission, remain counterevidence. The age addition is
not adopted: “ninety” survives in the published Qumran transcription, whereas
the following eight-year completion is supplied. No fresh letters were
deciphered, no ImageGen result was used as evidence, and no canon change follows.
