# Samuel numeral plate feasibility

The acquired DJD XVII plates do not supply a verified physical interval that
can distinguish four from forty at 2 Samuel 15:7. This additional inspection
leaves the [exact review's HOLD](SAMUEL_15_7_EXACT_REVIEW_2026-10-10.md) unchanged.
No numeral ink is recovered and no Hebrew or English record is changed.

## Source and inspection

The consulted primary edition is DJD XVII (2005), available through a
[nonpublisher-hosted consultation copy](https://messianicessenes.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/djd-17.pdf).
The [receipt](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/samuel15_7_plate_feasibility.2026-10-10.v1.json)
pins its bytes and locators. Neither the PDF nor rendered photographs are
redistributed here; redistribution rights have not been established.

Root visually inspected complete plate XIX (PDF 321), plate XXVI (PDF 331),
and printed pages 154, 264 and 265 (PDF 177, 287 and 288). Plate XIX identifies
4Q51 fragments 112-114a-b; plate XXVI identifies the 4Q53 column III fragments
7ii-11. The edition supplies the entire numeral/year phrase in 4Q51 line 11
and 4Q53 line 4. Its reported surviving traces near “king” in 4Q51 are not
surviving numeral letters, and are not a fresh reading made by this project.

The principal embedded plate rasters are 608 by 800 pixels. Rendering a page
at 1825 by 2400 pixels enlarges the existing material; it does not recover
original photographic detail. Publication montage spacing and typeset line
widths cannot substitute for an independently established physical gap.

## What this evidence can decide

The acquired images do not establish two surviving endpoints enclosing a
continuous, co-scaled numeral interval. No same-hand width calibration or
complete-line fit experiment was executed. Thus this result is not a finding
that both forms fit, that one form fails, or that all future geometry work is
impossible. The forms ארבעים שנה and ארבע שנים contain nine and eight letters
excluding spaces, respectively; unequal glyph widths, spacing and possible
orthographies prevent that count from deciding compatibility.

DJD printed page 265 does discuss reconstructed line lengths at 15:14. That
argument cannot be borrowed as a numeral-space measurement at 15:7. Its
15:7 preference for four is contextual and versional, not newly visible ink.

Stop this scan-only lane. Reopen only with physically mapped, original-resolution
photographs and surviving anchors, defensible fragment relationships and
same-hand spacing uncertainty, or a published target-specific geometry argument.
Compare complete reconstructions and preserve uncertainty; do not tune a fit
until four wins. ImageGen would not supply missing evidence.
