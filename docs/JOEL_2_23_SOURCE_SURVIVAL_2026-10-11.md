## Selected Greek and interpretation

The [German Bible Society's selected Greek chapter](https://www.die-bibel.de/bibel/LXX/JOL.2)
identifies Rahlfs's Septuagint, second revised edition by Hanhart, 2006.
At 2:23 its first gift uses **τὰ βρώματα εἰς δικαιοσύνην**, food/nourishment
language with righteousness, followed by early and latter rains.
**καθὼς ἔμπροσθεν** expresses the former/before comparison rather than an
explicit month. The chapter context, including 2:21–27, was read.

This is real selected Greek wording, not a complete critical apparatus or a
unique Hebrew retroversion. It neither gives direct teacher wording nor
proves that a different Hebrew noun stood in the exemplar. Compare local Greek
translation practice, relevant apparatus and lexical arguments before using it
to choose among Hebrew interpretations or change POB.
