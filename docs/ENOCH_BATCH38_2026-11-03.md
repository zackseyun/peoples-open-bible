# 1 Enoch repair batch 38

This individual application revises 1 Enoch 1:9 against the selected Charles
1906 print (p. 4, PDF 42). It retains the daggered judgment phrase and the
impiety verb as printed, with three notes distinguishing what is printed from
the provisional English and the editor's conjectures. The Greek and the
partially surviving Aramaic (4Q204) are comparative witnesses, not replacements
for the selected Geʿez text. No additional clause from Jude is imported.

The 2026-10-05 correction remains verifiable as a historical application.
Batch 38 binds its exact preimage, exact whole-unit review pair, and sole
canonical change. It does not approve chapter 1, which remains incomplete,
or certify a fresh manuscript collation. No publisher or release gate changes.
