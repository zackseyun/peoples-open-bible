# Obadiah reader disclosure and note anchors

The proposed application retains POB's Hebrew and main English for Obadiah 1:7
and 1:21. It correctly positions all six note anchors and replaces the verse 21
deliverer note with a qualified alternative: people who have been delivered.
This alternative changes the interpretation of the Hebrew vocalization, not
merely the style of English. Its disclosure does not select it as earliest.

The [source comparison](OBADIAH_1_7_21_SOURCE_COMPARISON_2026-10-10.md) establishes
the published readings and competing explanations. The
[frozen application contract](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/obadiah1_7_21_reader_contract.2026-10-10.v1.json)
pins both complete candidate records, baseline bytes, scope and review criteria
before independent assessment. No blind English preference or fresh two-provider
transcription is claimed.

## Exact changes and limits

In verse 7, the border note stays at border; the trap and pronoun notes move
from the preceding clause to their own referents. All note bodies and lexical
entries remain unchanged. The terse bread-fellowship interpretation is not
newly approved by this anchor repair.

In verse 21, notes attach to Deliverers, Esau's hill country and Yahweh's kingdom
clause respectively. The first note distinguishes the retained active Masoretic
reading, an alternative passive vocalization, and selected Greek saved people
coming from Zion. Published unpointed Murabba'at spelling does not decide voice.
The deliverer lexical rationale and its dependent theological rationale are
qualified accordingly; neither theology nor modern English agreement determines
source priority. The other lexical and theological entries remain historical,
not freshly reviewed or certified optimal.

Existing generation provenance and revision history remain intact. Present
historical review flags, English, notes and lexical or theological records are
copied exactly into review history, bound to the baseline hash and explicitly
not approval of the candidate. The current records will require further review
rather than carrying forward old agreement scores. Source wording, literary
form, main English, canon and About are not changed.

## Validation and application status

Preflight validates both complete candidates against the verse schema, checks
the exact historical archives and preserved fields, and exports the entire
one-chapter book with candidates overlaid. All 21 verses are present; only 7 and
21 differ, with all six notes exported and 19 other units unchanged. These
checks verify implementation and scope, not the scholarly truth of a reading.

One independent assessment passed the frozen candidates without material
defects. The exact reviewed records are applied locally; all four focused tests
pass, including the historical and current complete exports. All 37 shared
reader-note regression tests also pass, for 41 post-application tests. The
[application receipt](../sources/textual_restoration/applications/obadiah1_7_21_reader.2026-10-10.v1.json)
binds that result to the exact candidates and contract. Repository publication and a deployed reader
remain separate milestones, and no deployment is claimed.
The [continuation history](OT_SOURCE_COMPARISON_CONTINUATION_2026-10-04.md) retains
the actual review and delivery sequence, including unsuccessful attempts.
