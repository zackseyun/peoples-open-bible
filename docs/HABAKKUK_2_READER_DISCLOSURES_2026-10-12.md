# Habakkuk 2 reader disclosures and preserved review history

The proposed reader update makes two genuine ancient Hebrew alternatives
visible without replacing POB's source or main English. Habakkuk 2:5 discloses
wealth versus wine, including the changed following verb. Habakkuk 2:16
discloses the different letter order commonly understood as staggering, while
retaining the commentary's uncircumcised-heart counterargument. Both source
priority judgments remain HOLD. This is not an earliest-text selection or a
new English preference assessment.

## Notes and rationale changes

At 2:5, the new textual note follows **wine**. The old wine/betrayal lexical
note follows **betrays**, the rest/abiding note follows **rest**, and the
Sheol explanation follows **Sheol**. The three old note texts and reasons
are retained, with markers relabeled b, c and d. The delayed notes at satisfied
and the wine note after Moreover no longer describe unrelated phrases.
Removing all note markers leaves exactly the baseline English.

The wine lexical rationale is qualified: WLC's retained pointed form means
wine, but the ancient Hebrew quotation reads a different noun and verb form.
The modern selected Greek wine word is an editorial conjecture in the
consulted mediated apparatus, not independent manuscript agreement. Wealth
is a textual alternative, not a lexical gloss for the unchanged pointed noun.
Other lexical entries, theological metadata, philosophy and generation
provenance are retained, not newly certified. No fresh HALOT entry is claimed.

At 2:16, one new note follows **uncircumcision**, before the exclamation mark.
The source and marker-free English, including the existing divine-name wording,
remain exactly as before. The note reports an alternative rather than silently
translating the retained Hebrew as if its letters had been replaced.
