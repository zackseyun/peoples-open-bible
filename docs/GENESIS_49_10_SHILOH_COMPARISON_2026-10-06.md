## Evidence and alternative analyses

The current [verse](../translation/ot/genesis/049/010.yaml) says until Shiloh
comes. Its rationale relies partly on the OSHB proper-noun tag. The actual
[WLC XML](../sources/ot/wlc/Gen.xml) preserves ketiv `שילה` and qere
`שִׁיל֔וֹ`; both words carry `HNp`. That annotation records an analysis, not
independent historical evidence or a distinction between a person and a place.
The [existing inventory](../sources/textual_restoration/inventory/hebrew_qere/Gen.jsonl)
correctly keeps the written/read tradition separate from independent witnesses.
Neither form explicitly spells the relative expression whose right it is.
Root and a read-only Hebrew analyst inspect these inputs and the royal context
in 49:8–11. Context establishes dominion, not the identity of this difficult word.

Richard C. [Steiner's primary argument](https://media.alhatorah.org/1Bereshit/49/Poetic%20Forms%20-%20Steiner%20-%20JBL%20129.pdf),
JBL 129/2 (2010), 209–235, distinguishes ordinary repointing from `שִׁי לֹה`:
he proposes a poetic sound contraction preserving the Masoretic vowels, with
joined short words explaining the written unit. Root visually inspects complete
printed 219–226 and 234–235, including footnotes; the analyst reads the argument
independently. This is philological interpretation, not an attested new Genesis
spelling. At printed 221 note 64 he also proposes assigning עד to the preceding
hemistich with an eternity sense, deferring proof to a future article; that
must not be silently bundled into a narrow word decision. His appendix leaves
the Greek's underlying Hebrew analysis uncertain. These claims summarize his
argument, not our verification of every cited edition or inscription.

Root and the analyst check the five diagnostic WLC verses above, including
Isaiah 60:13. In the latter, Lebanon's glory can come: an inanimate valuable
subject does not automatically require changing Genesis' active arrival verb
to a passive. These parallels establish linguistic possibilities, not priority.

The [publisher's Greek body](https://www.die-bibel.de/bibel/LXX/GEN.49),
attributed to Rahlfs/Hanhart 2006, gives `ἕως ἂν ἔλθῃ τὰ ἀποκείμενα αὐτῷ`.
Its grammatical subject is the things reserved for him, not a named Shiloh or
simply he who owns it. Root reads the surrounding Judah passage and edition
footer. This selected Greek does not uniquely recover Hebrew segmentation;
no full Greek apparatus or individual manuscript collation is claimed.

The complete [published 4Q252 context](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q252/2026-05-21/index.html),
section 5 lines 1–5, links Judah's rule, the Davidic throne and covenant to a
righteous anointed figure, the shoot of David. Its interpretive expansion is
early reception evidence, not an exact continuous Genesis copy establishing
Shiloh's spelling. Supplied openings remain supplied. Attribution: DFG project
465277421, CC BY-SA 4.0. Root reads this context; no fresh ink is inspected.
The NET publisher's [translation note](https://classic.net.bible.org/passage.php?mode=print&passage=Gen+49%3A10&theme=wiki)
supplies competing analyses, not another ancient vote.

## Outcome and application boundary

The personal-name rationale is insufficient, but the inspected evidence does
not uniquely select tribute or rightful owner. Provisional retention preserves
the declared base while source interpretation remains held. Remove the modest
chronological preference and the ambiguity remains: an early interpretive
expansion cannot discriminate these underlying analyses. Reopen for a directly
consulted apparatus or argument that distinguishes segmentation and referent,
including contrary evaluation of the poetic-vowel account, not another model vote.

In current English, marker a follows Judah but explains Shiloh; marker b follows
Shiloh comes but explains obedience. A necessary candidate would put a after
Shiloh comes and b after obedience of the peoples, correct the circular rationale,
and disclose unresolved interpretation. That is not approval of a new main
reading. No canonical bytes, source label, review status or reader export change
in this pass; a full-record candidate and application check remain separate.
