# 1 Enoch 48: ten-unit source review, publication HOLD

Two independent read-only Codex passes checked all ten selected Charles1906
printed90-92/PDF128-130 units. Pass 2 used 400DPI primary images. The
chapter has ten canonical YAML files, but current 48:10 clips after the
rest-on-earth opening. Printed 48:10 continues over the page boundary before
XLIX with falling/not rising, hand/raising, denial of the Lord of Spirits and
his Anointed, and a final blessing. The critical apparatus reports variant
omissions. The expanded parser candidate establishes scope, not exact glyph or
witness approval.

Both passes hold 48:7, 48:8 and 48:10. High-resolution pass 2 also holds
48:1, 48:3, 48:6 and 48:9 over exact printed glyphs, apparatus signs and
English/footnote placement. This overturns any superficial inference that
chapter 48 is publication-near merely because ten YAMLs and draft art exist.
No source choice is made from a single model reading; further scan-bound
transcription, critical policy and whole-chapter editorial review are needed.

No canonical YAML, graphic artwork, catalog, app UI or full-book publication
was approved by this package. Inputs, results, provenance and image hashes
are preserved; source scan bitmaps remain outside Git.
