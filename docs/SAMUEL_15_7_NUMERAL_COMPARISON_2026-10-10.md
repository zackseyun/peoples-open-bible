correction changes spelling within forty, not the numeral. Lower Hexaplaric
report terminates in j: a manuscript marginal report, not an inspected
Hexapla autograph. Physical manuscript dates and all relationships remain
unverified. Swete volumeI (fourth1909, reprint1925) is the correct Samuel
control, not initially suggested volumeII; it is not an additional ancient vote.

## Case and countercase

Four fits a short interval following reconciliation/activity and is genuinely
attested in versions, not invented to repair English. But Hebrew15:7 states
**no epoch**; Josephus supplies reconciliation. David's regnal forty years
strain a forty-year interval since the nearby reconciliation, without proving
every possible chronology impossible. An unstated alternative epoch cannot be
inserted as if translated from Hebrew.

Secondary chronological smoothing is a strong countercase to four, supported
as a possible process by p's actual correction, not proved for every four
reading. Conversely, secondary numeral assimilation or another copying process
could explain forty, but no actual motive/mechanism was demonstrated.
Version dependence remains uncertain; numerical vote counts cannot decide.

`ארבעים שנה → ארבע שנים` changes both numeral and year noun. It is not a
proved single-glyph numerical confusion; four-year wording in a version does
not uniquely recover that Hebrew spelling, vowels or an autograph.

## What this contributes

The immediately valid result is an auditable comparison, including contrary
evidence and correction of apparent Qumran confirmation. If adopted, four
changes narrative duration; it is a known variant, not a novel discovered word.
Tokens can buy evidence acquisition and tests, not missing letters or guaranteed
original wording. This case provides no ground for canon expansion: age,
survival and quotation concern history/reception, while canon membership needs
a separately stated tradition and theological/editorial decision.
