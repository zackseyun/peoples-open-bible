## Direct source controls

Current Hebrew Numbers **4:23** and **8:24** explicitly combine the צבא root
with עבודה/עבד and the tent-of-meeting formula. They establish related service
language, not who copied whom, a dated redaction or a specific office for women.
Current Exodus **38:8** and Samuel **2:22** retain the previously identified
relationship. These four records were read and remain byte-identical to the
baseline; their modern English is context, not an independent manuscript vote.

The actual Brooke–McLean Exodus edition, printed **287–288 / PDF 145–146**,
aligns Hebrew **38:8** with selected Greek **38:26**. The Greek has
`τῶν νηστευσασῶν αἳ ἐνήστευσαν` and the continuation
`ἐν ᾗ ἡμέρᾳ ἔπηξεν αὐτήν`. Thus its selected wording involves fasting and an
erection-day expression, not an identical service clause. Root visually read
both complete pages and the edition's preface on PDF 9–11 as extracted text.
Its selected text can prefer corrections for apparatus convenience; it does
not certify the earliest Greek or an unaltered first-hand B at every word.

This positive Exodus comparison neither attests omission in Samuel nor proves
a different Hebrew spelling behind the Greek. It also does not independently
verify the modern paper's entire Latin or reviser argument. No fasting, visions,
coercion or priestess terminology is inserted into POB by this follow-up.
