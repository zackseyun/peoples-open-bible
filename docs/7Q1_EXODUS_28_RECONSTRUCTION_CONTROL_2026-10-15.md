# 7Q1 Exodus 28: a competing-reconstruction control

## Result

The original edition's relevant pages are now directly consultable, advancing
7Q1 beyond the [catalogue-only follow-up](JUDEAN_GREEK_SOURCE_FOLLOWUP_2026-09-05.md).
This opens a concrete restoration test; it does not recover a new letter.
No Hebrew source, English translation, note, manuscript count or canon changes.
4Q122's separate DJD IX edition-access gap remains open.

## Consulted evidence

Root and one acquisition agent visually read the public Google Books scans of
DJD III, [p.142](https://books.google.com/books?id=xFnOe0beecAC&pg=PA142&hl=en&output=html_text)
and [p.143](https://books.google.com/books?id=xFnOe0beecAC&pg=PA143&hl=en&output=html_text).
The first page supplies the 7Q1 transcription and physical description; the
next contains its notes before the separately headed 7Q2 entry. The edition
credits Boismard for the identification and much of the commentary. Its
heading specifies plate XXX; that printed plate was not obtained.

The edition describes approximately 3 mm letters, 6 mm interlines and 5.4 cm
written column width for 7Q1. It tentatively places fragment 2 close below
fragment 1 from fiber orientation. Crucially, p.143 explicitly says verses
6–7 in the intervening gap were completed from Vaticanus. Supplied words are
therefore not independent support for Vaticanus. The neighboring 7Q2 entry's
22-letter average and 8.9 cm width must not be imported into 7Q1's description.

Root also read complete rendered pp.1–2 of John William Wevers,
[*The Dead Sea Scrolls and the Septuagint*, BIOSCS 38 (2005)](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/static/ioscs/pdf/volumes/bioscs38.pdf).
At Exodus 28:7 he reports doubtful σον in one row and τέρα below (also with
uncertain letters), distinguishes
supplied wording, and disputes the necessity of the edition's Hebrew-like
word order. He argues that his alternative fits a 22-letter line. This is
his reconstruction argument, not our measured geometric result. His p.1
footnote also cautions against treating the damaged 28:4 brother-expression
as a reasonably certain variant. It concerns 7Q1, not 4Q122.

## Comparison and decision

| Comparison surface | Ordering at the disputed clause | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| DJD III reconstruction, as compared by Wevers | συνέχουσαι → ἔσονται αὐτῷ | Editorial completion around limited surviving traces |
| Wevers's alternative labeled LXX | ἔσονται αὐτῷ → συνέχουσαι | Competing reconstruction to test, not newly read ink |
| [German Bible Society's selected LXX text, Exodus 28:7](https://www.die-bibel.de/bibel/LXX/EXO.28) | συνέχουσαι → ἔσονται αὐτῷ | Directly consulted modern edition selection, not another manuscript |

The publisher text agrees in this order with the first proposal, not with
what Wevers calls LXX. Thus the label "LXX" alone is an inadequate comparator:
our record must identify the actual selected text and keep its apparatus
separate. No fresh Göttingen apparatus consultation is claimed.

Current POB retains WLC's `חֹבְרֹת יִהְיֶה לּוֹ` and renders the verse with
two shoulder pieces joined to the garment's ends. The Greek word-order
alternatives do not here establish different Hebrew consonants or a different
garment construction. Choosing one completed Greek line by how closely it
resembles this Hebrew would make the comparison circular. No English revision
is supported by this bounded result; whole-verse optimality is not certified.

## Next experiment, not yet performed

Acquire plate XXX or verified corresponding measured photographs, identify
both fragments, and map their rows to the edition. Freeze trace-only readings
and uncertainty before aligning either complete Greek candidate. Estimate
spacing from actual intact same-hand letters, with separate horizontal and
vertical error bounds; test both alignments and an abstention outcome. A raw
character count is not a physical width measurement, especially when several
letter identities and fragment placement are uncertain.

Reject any alleged unique restoration if both candidates remain compatible.
Report a changed decision only if a specified original-image trace or geometric
constraint discriminates them. A generated picture of either completed line
would illustrate a hypothesis, not corroborate it. No ImageGen call or
manuscript-image transcription was made in this pass. This is an operational
case for the existing [restoration method](TEXTUAL_ADJUDICATION_METHOD.md#imagegen-and-restoration),
not a newly validated image-recovery technique.
