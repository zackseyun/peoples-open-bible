# Esther papyrus 967 calendar controls

## Result and source text consequence

Papyrus 967 now supplies checked early Greek evidence for **Adar 14** at both
the casting result in 3:7 and Haman's intended execution date in Addition B.
The two numerals are visible in Kenyon's historical photographic plates, not
merely restored in a transcription. This closes the earlier target-access hold
at those two units. It does not establish the earliest Hebrew wording, justify
inserting a day into Hebrew 3:7, or harmonize Hebrew 3:13's thirteen to fourteen.
Current POB source, English and notes remain unchanged by this comparison.

The [evidence record](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/esther_p967_calendar.2026-10-16.v1.json)
pins the sources, locators, derivatives and current POB controls. This is a

## What this changes and what remains open

The prior selected-RH date contrast now has a specific early physical witness
behind these two Greek dates. It is no longer only a modern selected-text
comparison or a catalogue coverage possibility. The papyrus, its printed text
and its photographic plates remain **one witness**, not three independent votes.
The reading is already published, not a new discovery or a fresh blinded
decipherment. These scans reproduce historical photographs, not new spectral
captures or measured recovery of previously invisible ink.

The result does not decide whether the Greek day was added, a Hebrew day was
lost, or another textual history explains the alternatives. Hebrew 3:7 does
not state a chosen day; Hebrew 3:13 does state thirteen. Addition B is Greek
evidence, not recovered Hebrew. The [Alpha ending](ESTHER_8_9_SOURCE_COMPARISON_2026-10-16.md#direct-alpha-edition-and-its-numbering)
has its own Adar 13 control and cannot be silently blended with this form.

Nor does the papyrus prove a necessary preemptive attack. Its two dates do not
cover the countermanding instructions or later fighting dates. The previously
checked legal/defensive context remains necessary. The listed Dublin material
does not reach the chapter 9 targets, and these photographs do not change that
non-coverage into an omission. Hanhart's modern target apparatus at 9:10 and
9:16–17 remains unconsulted and is the next distinct edition input.
