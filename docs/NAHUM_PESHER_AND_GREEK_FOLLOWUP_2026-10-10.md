# Nahum pesher readings and recent Greek source routes

Two published Hebrew differences in 4Q169 belong inside Nahum quotations:
a repulsiveness expression at 3:6 and a city setting at 3:11. They are meaningful
source alternatives, not merely modern English synonyms. Neither currently
establishes earliest wording. POB's Hebrew and English remain provisionally
retained. This comparison expands the earlier
[Nahum screen](NAHUM_SOURCE_COMPARISON_2026-10-10.md) without claiming new
decipherment, comprehensive collation or a canon recommendation.

## Repulsiveness or spectacle at 3:6

[4Q169, fragments 3–4, column iii, lines 1–4](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q169/2026-05-21/index.html)
places the disputed word before its interpretation formula. The quotation word
is unbracketed and unmarked in this edition. The
[Kottsieper and Steudel lexicon entry כאר I, version 1.0.0 (2024), Forms and Semantics A–B](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/articles/1177/2024-04-29/de_DE/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
explicitly analyzes it as a feminine singular Qal-passive participle and
distinguishes the related Piel expression in the commentary. Its sense is
approximately “make you repulsive,” against POB's pointed Masoretic “make you
a spectacle.” This is not a “like light” segmentation or a harmless spelling
variant of the Masoretic noun.

The [Mur.88 column17 line13 reading](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/Mur._88/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
preserves only the shared initial kaf of the disputed word. Its following
letters are supplied: it cannot supply a preserved spectacle-ending vote.

The repulsiveness reading fits the preceding humiliation imagery. It also
echoes the commentary's treatment of opponents. Our inference is that this
echo permits two directions: an inherited reading explained by the commentator,
or an adaptation serving that explanation. The echo alone does not decide
direction. Conversely, spectacle fits the following seeing/onlookers language;
that contextual fit is not proof that the Masoretic form is earlier.

## Refuge with or without a city at 3:11

[4Q169 column iv lines6–8](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q169/2026-05-21/index.html)
has the city expression unbracketed before the partially preserved interpretation
formula. The quotation's preceding verb is supplied; the city noun is not.
The next commentary line repeats the city expression, with an uncertainty mark
on its final resh. Under this reading the clause would approximately say
“seek a refuge in a city from the enemy.” Definite versus indefinite city is
not determined by the unpointed spelling.

[Mur.88 column17 line20](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/Mur._88/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
supplies the entire refuge clause. Its reconstructed shorter wording is not
surviving evidence for the absence of a city. POB has the shorter Masoretic
clause. A clarifying city addition is a plausible explanation, but an inherited
longer quotation subsequently reused by the commentator remains possible.
No specific city, city-of-refuge institution or historical event follows.

## Greek controls and priority

The publisher's [selected Greek Nahum3](https://www.die-bibel.de/bibel/LXX/NAM.3)
has εἰς παράδειγμα at3:6 and στάσιν ἐξ ἐχθρῶν at3:11, without an explicit city
word. Root also visually reads Swete volumeIII (1905), printed p.56, PDF page80,
including its target apparatus. The first is an example/exposure expression,
not the pesher's explicit repulsiveness adjective. Broad semantic overlap,
translation or interpretation could still explain it; it does not uniquely
recover Hebrew letters. The shorter Greek refuge expression similarly cannot
prove an identical Hebrew exemplar.

Swete's limited apparatus records spelling/correction variants at the first
expression and other nearby variants, not a target repulsiveness adjective or
city phrase. Apparatus silence is not exhaustive agreement. The two selected
Greek controls are not two independent ancient votes. The adjacent Greek
hidden/overlooked difference at3:11 is not adjudicated by this city comparison.

Source priority remains HOLD pending actual BHS/BHQ and relevant Greek/DSS
apparatus, with local transmission/adaptation arguments for these precise units.
The already-known note misplacements at3:6 and3:11 are separate implementation
work, not evidence for choosing either source. No new note application is
bundled into this research packet.
