# Independent lexical controls, frozen before candidate

This is an independently made source-photo reading record, not TEI/OCR semantics. TEI supplied heads and column locators only. Actual Dillmann 1865 Latin page photographs in `lexicon/` were inspected through the whole relevant root/nominal entries, including their continuations. The Latin extracts below give the sense structure; they are NOT represented as exhaustive diplomatic transcriptions of every cited Ethiopic/Greek/Hebrew example or bibliography. The root asked for full entry transcription: that requirement is not fully discharged by these extracts, and is an additional reason NOT to approve a whole unit where such detail is decisive.

## Actual entries and continuations

* **መብረቅ**, cols 502 bottom–503 top, photographs pp501 and pp503: `subst., plerumque m., Pl. መባርቅ ... 1) fulgur, fulmen ... 2) rarius: fulgor, (nimbus Sanctorum capita circumdans, Lud.)`. Both portions read to the next entry (በርበር). The ordinary lightning/flash noun has plural መባርቅ. The rare radiance/halo extension is not a warrant to replace the ordinary sense here. Related parent **በረቀ** at col502 is `1) fulgere, (micare, effulgere, splendere, coruscare); 2) fulgurare`. Its causative/derived entry is `fulgere vel splendere facere`, also `fulminare` and `fulmina emittere vel fulmina instar emittere`; reciprocal `simul vel alternis fulgurare`. These derivative senses are not themselves in the selected body.

* **ቆመ**, begins col451 bottom, continues col452, ends col453 before አቆመ. Photographs pp451 and pp453 read in that order. Latin sense structure: `stare, se sistere`; (1) `stare i.e. consistere, insistere, subsistere, inniti`, also `adstare, circumstare`, `locum tenere, persistere`, `praepositum esse, praeesse`, `resistere, insistere, obsistere`, `assistere, adesse alicui protegendo vel defendendo`; (2) `perdurare, constare` with `manere`, `ratum esse vel fieri`, `confirmari`, `exsistere`; then col453 `stabiliri`, `surgere vel prodire facere`, `constituere`, `stabilire i.e. confirmare, ratum facere`; (3) `sistere, subsistere facere; cohibere, inhibere, detinere`, also `sistere, absistere, cessare`, `desistere vel desinere`. These cover the whole root entry, not all ensuing derived nouns/verbs. It is not permissible to infer a lexical meaning 'transform' solely from this head.

* **ኀደገ**, cols615 lower half–616 entire–617 upper left, photographs pp615 and pp617. Read to the start of causative አኅደገ; no derivative's semantics silently applied to body. `Subj. ይኅድግ`. Latin structure: `A) transitive 1) relinquere` a) `discedere ab aliquo`, b) `in discedendo relinquere alqd`, c) `deserere, destituere`; `2) committere alicui alqd`; `3) dimittere` a) `rem vel personam, quam alqs tenebat`, b) `liberum dimittere, missum facere`, c) `dimittere uxorem i.e. repudiare, divortium facere`; `4) remittere, condonare` a) `debitum`, b) `peccatum`, rarely `expiare`; `5) omittere, praetermittere, intermittere` a) `omittere, praetermittere`, including the imperative used parenthetically with force `nedum, multo magis vel multo minus`, b) `negligere, curam rei vel pers. abjicere`, c) `intermissionem facere operis`, d) `missum facere, desistere vel abstinere a re, cessare facere alqd, finem facere rei, desinere`, with Acc. rei, Acc. Inf., or infinitive introduced by preposition; `6) sinere, permittere`, taking Acc. personae and appropriate subordinate verb or infinitive; `B) intransitive 1) remittere, cessare ... cedere alicui in re, eo inferiorem esse`. The inflectional and syntax discussion is substantial; a bare 'remain' rendering is not directly the head gloss. This root is only a proposed control until the exact printed penultimate form and its morphology are secure. The selected printed form must NOT be replaced by the apparatus's expressly 'normal' form.

* **ክህለ**, col812, photograph pp811, read from head through end at bottom of col812; photograph pp813 inspected to verify that following ተክህለ is a different derived head. Morphological lines include imperfect forms and `Subj. ይክል`. Sense structure: `posse, valere (pollere)`, `non possum officium implere`, `quantum potes (pro virium ratione)`, `efficere posse`, `posse per legem i.e. licere alicui`; `praevalere, superare`; `potestatem habere alicujus`, followed by a causative `potentem vel valentem facere alqm i.e. vires, potentiam, facultatem praebere alicui`; and causative secondary `praevalere facere, superiorem reddere alqm aliquo`. The selected `ወኢይክል` is negative third-person singular ability language, not a license to invent plural agreement or a future temporal marker.

* **ኮከብ**, col865 bottom–866 first lines, photograph pp865, read from head through end before next entry. `subst., plerumque m., et coll., Pl. ከዋክብት ... stella (sidus, astrum)`; examples include the constellation, bright star, heavenly stars, `stella crinita, cometes`, adjectival / ornament uses, concluding `martyres ... apostoli`. Here become/become lightning is lexically possible, but agreement and subject anchoring cannot be skipped.
