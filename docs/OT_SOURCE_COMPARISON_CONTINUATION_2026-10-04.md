# Old Testament source comparison method and continuation review

Reviewed 2026-10-04 against repository revision
`fff3aabab3f993bdeaf66c4bc8c8d1278def8dc9`. This records the continuation
decision, not a new complete manuscript census or approval of every POB verse.
The existing [adjudication method](TEXTUAL_ADJUDICATION_METHOD.md) remains
controlling; this document makes its execution and success criteria explicit.

The full objective remains to find relevant surviving Old Testament sources,
investigate recoverable wording, compare witnesses, establish the best-supported
attainable Hebrew/Aramaic corpus, and assess the effect on POB English. A bounded
pass is a work unit toward that objective, not a substitute for its full scope.
NT Greek and the reception of additional books are related but distinct tracks.

## Current findings and readiness

The current repository provisionally selects four cubits and a span in
[1 Samuel 17:4](../translation/ot/1_samuel/017/004.yaml) and includes light in
[Isaiah 53:11](../translation/ot/isaiah/053/011.yaml). Both records disclose
competing readings and remain drafts requiring review. These are applications
of published manuscript evidence to POB, not newly deciphered readings, recovered
autographs, or proof of public reader deployment. The numeral affects Goliath's
portrayal, not a demonstrated historical measurement; light alone establishes
neither resurrection nor the servant's identity.

The project can continue using published editions now. New damaged-ink claims
remain limited by the [unfinished calibration](TEXTUAL_ADJUDICATION_METHOD.md#calibration-and-review):
the four-region observation pilot produced no character-accuracy result. Two
agreeing models are not independent ancient witnesses. A newer model does not
erase prior disagreements or certify an earlier review.

More computation can increase comparison coverage and expose overlooked errors.
It cannot guarantee a new scholarly discovery, recover physically absent marks,
or establish inspiration. No corpus-wide translation improvement rate or uniquely
optimal English translation has been demonstrated. Fidelity and appropriate
uncertainty, not maximal asserted confidence, are the objectives.

## Source discovery across the full corpus

Use the existing [coverage audit](BIBLICAL_SOURCE_COVERAGE_AUDIT_2026-09-04.md)
and registry rather than building another inventory. For every OT book, retain
a dated record of applicable catalogue and edition checks. Distinguish sources
identified, accessible, passage-mapped, collated and adjudicated; none of these
statuses implies the next. Preserve unknown denominators and explicit exclusions.

Required source classes are early Masoretic codices and Genizah fragments;
Judaean Desert Hebrew/Aramaic manuscripts, including non-Qumran finds; Samaritan
Pentateuch witnesses; Old Greek/Septuagint manuscripts and appropriate critical
apparatuses; relevant Latin, Syriac and Aramaic versions; and quotations,
inscriptions, excerpts and retellings whose wording can discriminate a question.
An excerpt is not a continuous book copy, but should not be discarded for that
reason alone. New discoveries, reassigned fragments and corrected editions
require dated updates, not a claim that an unknowable final census is complete.

Record physical object, hand, shelfmark, passage actually surviving, language,
copy-date range, edition and access/rights basis. Resolve joins and duplicate
hosting before counting objects. Do not give a medieval copy the ancient date
of its translation. A catalogue range is not a surviving-letter inventory.

Systematic coverage has two complementary passes: book-by-book mapping and
comparison of available textual units, and focused adjudication of differences
that might affect source wording or English. Include a frozen sample of
unflagged passages when evaluating general translation quality; selected dramatic
variants cannot establish a whole-corpus improvement rate. Record omissions in
coverage so prioritization never masquerades as completeness.

## Evidence and restoration

Keep the archival image, reproducible non-generative derivatives, diplomatic
transcription and supplied reconstruction separate. Record image identity,
region and processing steps. Inspect originals alongside enhancements. Preserve
uncertain strokes, gaps, corrections and supplies instead of normalizing them
into apparently observed letters.

Published readings may be compared without a new transcription of every image.
Fresh decipherment, disputed strokes, joins or hand claims require actual image
evidence and the existing blinded, two-family workflow. Freeze separate outputs
before reconciliation and measure performance on varied, manuscript-disjoint
controls before broad acceptance. Contextual completion remains a hypothesis;
agreement does not turn it into ink. Until calibrated, fresh machine readings
must not be presented as demonstrated discoveries.

ImageGen can illustrate an already recorded reading or reconstruction. It cannot
recover absent ink, invent a spectral exposure, validate a letter or contribute
to source selection. Any illustration must be visibly labeled, separately
stored and excluded from OCR, collation and confidence assessment. Do not
generate an image merely to satisfy a workflow step when it answers no reader
need. No ImageGen output was produced in this continuation.

## Comparison and source selection

Declare a pinned base and target literary form for each comparison. Align actual
content rather than verse numbers alone. Retain exact witness wording in the
diplomatic layer and separately map normalization. A physical gap is not an
attested omission. A modern edition is not another ancient manuscript.

Compare serious alternatives using preservation, local scribal or translation
behavior, relationships, chronology and explanations of transmission. Record
the strongest objection as well as the preferred account. Older physical
witnesses receive the existing modest preference, never an automatic victory.
Do not use witness counts, shorter wording, difficulty or theological appeal
as a mechanical decision rule.

For versions, establish their own wording before asking what source reading it
can distinguish. A proposed back-translation must remain distinct from attested
Hebrew/Aramaic. Where literary forms genuinely differ, preserve parallel forms
or disclose the chosen target; do not silently manufacture a hybrid. The source
corpus is a transparent editorial product, not proof that its complete wording
once existed in a single ancient manuscript.

End each case with retain, propose change or unresolved. Separately explain
certainty about attestation, source priority, linguistic interpretation and
English rendering. State the concrete evidence that could reopen a hold.

## English application and reader verification

Compare current POB, a close gloss and the proposed rendering in paragraph
context. Assess lost meaning, unsupported additions, preserved ambiguity,
literary effect and readability. A change of vocalization or grammatical
analysis is not merely an English style edit. Unchanged source and English,
with a better disclosure or a justified hold, can be a valid outcome.

A proposed change needs an exact before/after source and English record, actual
review output, preserved competing readings and honest provenance. Apply only
the reviewed scope. Archive stale approval-looking metadata rather than reuse
it. Verify schemas, source/English preservation as applicable, reader notes and
book export coverage. Repository application and public deployment are separate
claims. Tests establish software behavior, not the historical truth of a reading.

## Efficient continuation and stopping rules

The [existing priorities](TEXTUAL_RESTORATION_PRIORITIES.md) already record
excessive historical expenditure relative to demonstrated results. Continue
with evidence-driven passes, not an unlimited judge-until-agreement loop.
Before each pass, specify the consequential question, missing discriminating
evidence, expected source/English effect and stopping point. Reuse consulted
sources; perform one bounded independent review, repeating only for a concrete
correction. Record disagreement rather than manufacturing consensus.

Do not reacquire held evidence without a changed access route or specific new
resource. Deuteronomy 32:8 retains its [recorded acquisition hold](DEUT32_8_EVIDENCE_TRIAGE_2026-09-06.md).
Image calibration remains paused unless a translation-relevant question requires
fresh image reading; this does not waive its gate for future decipherment.
Missing evidence in one case does not block other books or source classes.

This pass advances the existing [Nahash comparison](SAMUEL_NAHASH_COMPARISON_2026-09-07.md#greek-transition-apparatus-follow-up--2026-10-04)
through an available Greek apparatus. It shows why silence and month wording
must not be treated as mutually exclusive across Greek transmission. Neither
source nor main English is changed. The remaining Hebrew reading-note gate
stays explicit; no broad Nahash reacquisition is justified by this result.

## Additional books and canonical reception

Textual recovery and canonicity are different questions. Antiquity, quotation,
copy frequency or inclusion in an ancient codex can inform reception history;
none alone establishes inspiration or authorizes changing POB's book list.
Sirach and Tobit already occur in the [Catholic canon](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_one/section_one/chapter_two/article_3/iv_the_canon_of_scripture.html);
Enoch and Jubilees occur in the [Ethiopian Orthodox list](https://www.ethiopianorthodox.org/english/canonical/books.html?lang=en).
Describe whose canon is under discussion rather than assume one universal list.

An overlapping Sirach passage is a recommended bounded comparison because
ancient and medieval Hebrew witnesses can overlap; the [Ben Sira manuscript
project](https://bensira.org/introduction.html) identifies this evidence. Enoch
and Jubilees also warrant study, keeping fragmentary early-language attestation
separate from later complete versions. These are comparison recommendations,
not new collation results or canonical determinations from this pass.

Append actual sources and locators, observations, competing explanations,
decisions, source/English effects, checks, failed access and reopening conditions
to the [central research log](TEXTUAL_RESTORATION_RESEARCH_LOG.md). Preserve
historical records rather than overwrite their dates or fabricate undocumented
deliberation. A later About summary should link this evidence and state its limits.

The full goal remains incomplete: systematic all-book coverage, relevant
passage-level comparisons, calibrated fresh-reading claims where needed,
source/English decisions and verification cannot be certified by the current
pilots. This continuation makes progress without claiming those missing results.
