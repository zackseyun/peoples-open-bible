# Habakkuk witness coverage and Greek preservation followup

Newly consulted published Greek preservation does not distinguish the disputed
address at Habakkuk 1:5. A broader coverage check also identifies an important
search limitation: the nonbiblical QDR snapshot contains no verse annotations.
Its zero reference matches cannot exclude quotations. Source priority remains
HOLD, with no further Hebrew, main English or reader-note change.

## Greek preservation beyond the selected text

The publisher's public [Habakkuk preview](https://brill.com/display/book/edcoll/9789047407294/B9789047407294_s015.xml)
in Biblia Qumranica volume 3 B, 2005, exposes printed pages 127–128 of the
chapter by R. E. Fuller, A. Lange and E. Tov. The complete Greek synopsis on
page 128 and the enlarged 1:5–7 region were inspected independently by root
and the acquisition agent. This is newly consulted evidence, not newly
discovered material or a complete reading of chapter pages 127–139.

At 1:5 the 8ḤevXIIgr column has an isolated **]Α[**, a partial astonishment
word and portions of the work/reporting clauses. No identifiable surviving
address selects despisers or nations. This is inconclusive preservation for
that contrast, not an attested omission. In the reporting verb, **ΗΘΗ** is
printed as surviving and the preceding letters are reconstructed; the
conjunction before that verb is also supplied. The complete passive verb must
not be counted as preserved merely because its ending survives.

The freely accessible [introduction](https://brill.com/display/book/edcoll/9789047407294/front-2.xml),
ix–xv, identifies the second Greek column as a combination of the diplomatic
and reconstructed texts of DJD VIII's corrected 1995 edition. Root read the
complete introduction and visually checked xii–xiv; the
[editorial signs](https://brill.com/display/book/edcoll/9789047407294/front-3.xml),
xvi–xvii, were read and visually checked completely. Capitals and reconstructed
lettering, supply brackets and transformed damaged-character signs remain
distinct. Synoptic alignment does not reproduce physical gaps, word divisions
or vacat lengths. A highlighted empty comparison box is not proof of omission.

Fox's [publisher-hosted Proverbs volume](https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Proverbs_Fox_SBL.pdf),
copyright 2015, printed 32–33 (PDF 54–55), offers a separate scholarly discussion
of the same scroll's reporting-verb passive. The complete two-page discussion
and continuing footnote 5 were visually checked. This is an interpretation
control, not another manuscript or the actual Habakkuk critical apparatus.
The PDF's 2025 production timestamp is not the book's publication date.
The passive is compatible with the retained Hebrew reporting clause; it
does not establish the original address or Hebrew pointing by itself.

## Apparatus acquisition status and next evidence

The official [BHQ Twelve Minor Prophets product and preview](https://shop.die-bibel.de/Biblia-Hebraica-Quinta-BHQ-.-Band-13-Twelve-Minor-Prophets/5273)
identify Gelston's 2010 fascicle. Its 18-page preview includes front matter and
Hosea samples, not Habakkuk's target apparatus. The target entry was not
acquired. The Greek agent's Internet Archive DJD record marks its full PDF/OCR
private; no restricted content was requested.

Root's ordinary browser visit to the [announced Albrecht revision](https://www.vandenhoeck-ruprecht-verlage.com/57835/septuaginta-band-13)
confirms a substantive revising program, including Nahal Hever and apparatus
changes. But the same record simultaneously labels a fifth updated 2024 edition,
says publication is forthcoming in November 2024, gives approximate extent,
and disables purchase/ebook availability. That inconsistent record does not
establish a published, available 2024 apparatus. Confirm title/copyright and
availability before treating it as the current consulted edition.

Next acquire DJD VIII's corrected target diplomatic/reconstructed pages,
reconstruction commentary and plate, plus the actual Hebrew and Greek locus
apparatus with conventions. Until then, the new Greek ending and unindexed
coverage diagnosis strengthen the method but do not justify replacing source
letters. No ImageGen evidence, canon change or deployed-reader claim follows.
