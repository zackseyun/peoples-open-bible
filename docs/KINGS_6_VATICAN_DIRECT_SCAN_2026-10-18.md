# Kings 6 direct Vatican scan and verification limits

## Decision

The public digitization of Vatican Greek 1238 is now acquired for direct
comparison, but this pass establishes no secure new transcription of
1 Kings 6:1. Retain the prior provisional Hebrew and English. The published
Holmes–Parsons report for 246 remains edition evidence; it has not become
a photograph-confirmed 480 through this inspection.

## Manuscript and image identity

The [current Göttingen record for Ra 246](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/catalogue/Ra_246/)
identifies Vatican Greek 1238, dated 1195, with Reigns I–IV at 232r–330v,
in a manuscript now bound in three volumes. This matches the shelfmark in
HP's own catalogue. The record links the [Vatican digitization](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Vat.gr.1238.pt.1).
Catalogue identity and date do not independently date the target reading.

The [part 2 manifest](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiif/MSS_Vat.gr.1238.pt.2/manifest.json)
has 141 canvases; the [part 3 manifest](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiif/MSS_Vat.gr.1238.pt.3/manifest.json)
has 143. Their numeric canvas labels are image positions, not folio numbers.
Part 2 images 5, 70 and 135 were sampled to locate the historical books.
Part 3 complete openings 43–46 and one native-size crop of opening 44 were
then inspected. These sampled openings are not a whole-manuscript collation.

For opening 44, the image service reports 2304 by 1809 native pixels.
The crop requests the region `200,600,950,850`, without enlargement,
generative reconstruction or contrast alteration. Display scaling must not
be used as native coordinates: a point at crop y225 would be full-image
y825, not y640. No exact manuscript folio is certified here.

## Reading result and withdrawn identification

Heavy overlapping ink makes the relevant area difficult to interpret.
An independent reader initially proposed a tentative verb and target
location, while declining to read the numeral securely. A coordinate and
surrounding-word check did not substantiate that clause identification.
The reader withdrew both the target-specific verb and the coordinates.
Root's tentative visual impression is also not accepted as a transcription.

The [adjudication method](TEXTUAL_ADJUDICATION_METHOD.md) now explicitly requires
neighboring-word anchoring and a native-coordinate/crop mapping before accepting
a target-specific image reading. It allows genuinely contextless fragments
to be identified as such, rather than inventing surrounding words.

The result is therefore an unverified target, not a partial secure collation
and not confirmation of either forty or eighty. No scribal hand, correction
or textual layer was distinguished. The visible interference alone does not
establish whether it is underlying writing, show-through or another effect.
The catalogue's description of substantial palimpsest material does not
identify the cause at this particular passage.

## Canon and next evidence

The same Ra 246 catalogue lists the Testament of Job at 340r–349v and the
Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs at 350r–379v. These are candidates for
comparative study, not newly discovered works or grounds by themselves for
changing the canon. A bound collection's contents do not establish the
authority or reception of every work it contains. These passages were not
read or dated independently in this pass.

Stop rereading these same low-resolution openings. A secure direct reading
now needs a better target locator and legible source evidence or an explicit
critical collation. Enlarging these pixels or generating plausible strokes
would not supply new evidence. No further same-scan acquisition loop follows.

Vatican photographs remain private study assets. Its site requires permission
for printed or online image reproduction; no photographs are committed or
published. No source, English, canon, reader, publisher or About edit follows.
