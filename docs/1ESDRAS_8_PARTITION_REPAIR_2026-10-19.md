# 1 Esdras 8: repairing the printed-source import

The chapter's digital source lost two passages already present in its selected
printed edition. This repair restores the purpose clause in 8:25 and the oath
event in 8:92, and realigns the intervening split/combined units. It does not
discover ancient ink, select an earlier literary form, recover Hebrew or change
the canon. The birth/dismissal priority question in the
[preceding comparison](EZRA_10_MARRIAGE_ENDING_COMPARISON_2026-10-19.md) stays open.

## Evidence and exact scope

The underlying scan is Swete, volume II, printed pp.150–158, PDF pp.168–176,
SHA256 `945c5b15bf0f9dfc93890b28ee5b66a388acbf4597f1f2be5430ac6cba9c30b0`.
That hash matches the source recorded in the repository's
[Swete manifest](../sources/lxx/swete/MANIFEST.md). The nine page identities
are also checked against the stored image-response hashes. Raw filenames
`vol2_p0167`–`vol2_p0175` are zero-based scan-leaf indices, not printed pages.
The legacy edition key `lxx-swete-1909` is retained for compatibility; it is not
newly verified publication-date evidence.

The source author and root each inspect all nine complete page images for the
chapter boundaries, including the surrounding apparatus. This is a boundary
and omission check, not a fresh token-by-token edition of the chapter.

The [frozen diagnostic](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/1esdras8_partition_diagnostic.2026-10-19.v1.json)
records the complete chapter map and the historical-layer pins. Its first
boundary mismatch is at13/14; sustained offset first appears at40. The exact
[Greek candidate](../sources/textual_restoration/candidates/1esdras8_source_partition.2026-10-19.v1.json)
has 92 printed units. The
[English candidate](../sources/textual_restoration/candidates/1esdras8_english_partition.2026-10-19.v1.json)
and [complete-record application](../sources/textual_restoration/candidates/1esdras8_application.2026-10-19.v1.json)
cover 46 target IDs: 13,14,17,18,25,39–62,76–92. Target 81 retains its Greek but
belongs to the affected English/metadata context. No general Greek spelling
polish or English style revision is intended.

Root's complete-image check finds one correction to the frozen scout map:
inline verse84 begins at `ἔδωκας`, not at the preceding address to the Lord.
The address and participial clause therefore finish verse83. The exact source
and English candidates correct that boundary while leaving the earlier
diagnostic unchanged as a record of the initial assessment.

Two losses are substantive:

- 8:25 adds `δοξάσαι τὸν οἶκον αὐτοῦ τὸν ἐν Ἰερουσαλήμ`, the purpose of
  glorifying his house in Jerusalem. It survives raw transcription, parsing
  and the ours-only stream, then disappears when adjudication replaces the
  unit with its opening. The awkward preceding `μου` is not silently deleted.
- 8:92 restores Ezra administering an oath to the priestly and Levitical
  leaders/all Israel and their swearing, immediately before the chamber scene
  in9:1. It survives raw transcription but is absent already in parsed AI.
  All 92 digital verse numbers nevertheless remain present.

The model's internal reason for either omission is not established. No other
whole-clause omission/duplication was found by this boundary comparison; that
is not a token-level lexical collation of the chapter or a guarantee that the
edition has the earliest ancient reading.

## Application and historical review boundaries

Keep raw transcriptions, parsed records, ours-only records and the adjudicated
book unchanged. Replay the exact correction into the existing normalized
override lane with:

```sh
.venv/bin/python tools/build_normalized_deuterocanon_corpus.py --book 1ES
```

The scoped option leaves other normalized books and their summary untouched.
The loader already prefers a normalized book when present; it must therefore
return the repaired Greek on future source loads, rather than regenerate the
same omission. The generator rejects drift in the historical source hash or
unexpected changes outside the reviewed chapter units.

The canonical application moves/splits/joins the corresponding English,
restores both missing passages, routes notes to the clauses they explain and
updates active lexical/theological decisions. Artificial-fragment explanations
and false comparisons generated by approximate Hebrew alignment are not kept
as current evidence. Unverified approximate Hebrew parallels are archived with
the prior source fields, not presented as newly checked witnesses. This does
not deny real Hebrew/Aramaic parallels or establish a Hebrew Vorlage.

Old provider receipts and review fields remain historical. Each changed record
archives its previous source, English, decisions and review state with baseline
commit/file hash. The original AI draft receipt stays intact, not relabeled as
a receipt for this revision. Active records return to `draft`/`needs_review`;
source confidence is `medium`, and old high-agreement labels do not certify the
new record. Exact partition review is narrower than complete verse approval.

Three old target shells (41,85,87) had no `ai_draft` at all. Their corrected text
comes from old42,86,88, respectively. Those actual contributing records' original
receipts are relocated unchanged and explicitly labeled with origin ID/path,
baseline hash, the old target's missing-receipt status and
`certifies_current_record: false`. The source note discloses the relocation.
This supplies real historical provenance, not a fabricated new provider call
or evidence that the revised record was model-reviewed.

## Validation and publication

The focused regression checks complete-record schema, exact scope and archives,
all note-marker bindings, original-input pins, normalized-source reproducibility
and the actual source loader. A real before/after run of the whole-book exporter
over all 496 canonical files must retain every unrelated output and include
both restored events. Contiguous numbering alone is deliberately insufficient.
The baseline exports eight chapters and 425 units, not all nine chapters:
chapter5 has records1–70 and an isolated145 in both canonical YAML and the
historical source, so the exporter withholds it under its gap policy. This
pre-existing defect remains unchanged by the chapter8 repair and requires a
separate printed-source alignment, not deletion of145 or fabricated missing
units. A complete 1Esdras reader export is not achieved by this batch. The
[application evidence packet](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/1esdras8_application.2026-10-19.v1.json)
pins the exact reviewed inputs; one bounded independent review checks the
assembled source/English/application before canonical replacement.

The reader and publisher layout remain on hold following the user's concern
that inline disclosure can be messy. No panels or new disclosure system
are introduced by this source repair. A possible future direction is a small
marker only for meaning-bearing variants with full details on demand, but this
is a proposal, not authorization or an implemented design. Existing notes are
only routed to their corrected passages here.

Publication must use an ordinary commit/merge/push and the exact new head's
successful integrity checks. A source repair in Git is not proof that a separate
website/CDN deployment has refreshed.
