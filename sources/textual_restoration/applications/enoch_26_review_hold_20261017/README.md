# 1 Enoch 26: six-unit scan review, source HOLD

Two independent read-only Codex passes checked all six selected Charles1906
printed64-66/PDF102-104 units, the facing Greek/apparatus page and 400DPI
primary images in pass 2. They agree on the source chapter boundary: 26:4
continues across the page through the three-mountain clause, followed by
numbered 26:5-6 before XXVII. Current POB 26:4 clips after “another”, and
26:5-6 are absent although the OCR places them in ch27.txt.

Both passes hold 26:4's valley/mountain transcription and translation. The
second also holds 26:3 at an uncertain source form; its reading differs from
the first pass. The passes differ on whether 26:1 can be revised directly and
whether 26:2 is fully accepted. No consensus is fabricated. Critical brackets,
parentheses, asterisks and apparatus-dependent editorial additions must not
be flattened into unqualified running narrative. The facing Greek differs on
water direction and is comparison evidence, not silent Ethiopic replacement.
No modern topography or invented forest is asserted.

No canonical YAML, graphic artwork, catalog, app UI or full-book publication
was approved by this package. Fresh scan-bound repairs and whole-chapter
editorial review are needed before source application. Inputs, results,
provenance and image hashes are preserved; scan bitmaps remain outside Git.
