# Second unflagged OT English sample contract

Declared October 6, 2026 before executing selection or inspecting its winners.
Seed: `POB-unflagged-2026-10-06-v2`. Select one verse in each of Torah,
Prophets and Writings, without redraws. This is a source-stable English audit,
not an earliest-Hebrew selection, fresh transcription or canon assessment.

## Selection and preservation

Reuse the eligibility, pointed-spelling normalization, three book strata and
SHA256 path ranking in the immutable
[first selector](../tools/textual_restoration/build_unflagged_english_sample.py)
and its [contract](UNFLAGGED_ENGLISH_SAMPLE_PREDECLARATION_2026-09-05.md).
The second runner substitutes only the declared seed and records its actual
protocol inputs and Git revision. Freeze the complete population denominators,
source hashes, winners and input hashes before semantic assessment.

Do not exclude or redraw a winner because its wording is uninteresting,
uncertain or previously reviewed. Record any overlap with the first sample.
All POB verses have prior model drafting/review exposure; this is a new draw,
not a manuscript-disjoint or project-unseen held-out benchmark. Unflagged is
the selector's local category, not absence of apparatus variants. Keep the
first experiment and protected shared method unchanged.

## Assessment and contribution gate

Read each selected whole chapter in POB and its Hebrew source; identify the
relevant literary unit and make a close gloss. Assess meaning/agency, omissions
or unsupported additions, preserved ambiguity, literary function, naturalness
and consistency in that order. Consult passage-specific published source
controls where needed and distinguish actual consultation from existing YAML
lexicon labels. Pin the inspected context files and actual reference evidence.

One assessor handles each stratum. Assessors see POB and its existing rationale:
no blind candidate identity or model-independent calibration is claimed.
Compare a concrete candidate only where there is a stated improvement question.
Record retain/tie, propose change or unresolved, with the strongest objection,
source-interpretation limits and reopening condition. Do not force changes.
One bounded critic checks the combined findings; disagreements remain evidence,
not a reason to repeat judging until approval.

Report all three outcomes and candidate regressions, without an OT-wide error
or improvement percentage. A proposed meaning improvement needs an identifiable
gain and no known semantic regression; equivalent stylistic alternatives are
ties. Source selection, reader application and publication approval remain
separate gates. No canonical verse change is authorized by sample selection
alone. Bind any later application to its exact candidate and actual export.
