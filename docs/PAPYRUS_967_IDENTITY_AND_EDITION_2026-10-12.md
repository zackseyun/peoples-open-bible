## One codex across catalogue labels and holdings

[Göttingen](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/catalogue/Ra_967/) identifies
record1490, ridRa_0967, despite its empty structured content overview. The
[Cologne custodial account](https://papyri.uni-koeln.de/features/rahlfs) reports
approximately200CE, two copying hands and portions at Cologne, Dublin,
Princeton and Barcelona. It provides page/line locators for Ezekiel, pre-Hexaplaric
Old Greek Daniel, Bel, Susanna and Greek Esther. Physical-copy dating does not
date the translation, composition or origin of every reading.

Kenyon's [1937 text introduction](https://chesterbeatty.ie/assets/uploads/2018/11/The-Chester-Beatty-Biblical-Papyti-Descriptions-and-Texts-of-Twelve-Manuscripts-on-Payprus-of-the-Greek-Bible.pdf),
printedv–xii, explains the former separate Chester Beatty IX/X descriptions.
Its [1938 plate-preface](https://chesterbeatty.ie/assets/uploads/2018/11/The-Chester-Beatty-Biblical-Papyri-Descriptions-and-Texts-of-Twelve-Manuscripts-on-Papyrus-of-The-Greek-Bible-Fasciculus-VII.pdf),
printedv–vi, supports reunification through conjugate Ezekiel/Esther leaves,
the Daniel/Esther hand and pagination. It corrects Princeton's leaf count from
26 to21 and qualifies Ezekiel19:12–39:29 with five missing leaves. The reported
codex-page spans are39–40,43–56,59–64,67–74,79–90; a broad passage span is not
uninterrupted surviving text. The 1937 calculation must not replace that correction.

One registry row is added; all41 prior mixed witness/family/edition rows remain
unchanged. The new42-row total is not a count of42 independent manuscripts.

## Daniel service wording and the missing comparison

Kenyon1937 printed27/PDF45 publishes folio77recto, codex153, through Daniel7:11,
then a gap; the next portion, folio77verso/codex154, begins within7:14. **Verse13
is not transcribed in this Beatty portion.** Its absence here is a physical
coverage limit, not an attested omission or evidence against either comparison
or destination wording. The royal-authority opening of14 is likewise outside
this consulted portion.

The published remainder includes the letter sequence `λατρευουσα αυτω`
outside supply brackets, following the glory expression. Kenyon's local
apparatus calls that service construction *sic* and contrasts the Chigi reading
with added wording. This supports the [earlier review's](DANIEL_7_SOURCE_FORMS_COMPARISON_2026-10-10.md)
distinction between Greek service syntax and the Aramaic peoples' service; it
does not authorize importing either construction into the other language.
Accent normalization or reading reconstructed prefixes is not claimed here.

The complete surrounding printed27–28 pages and apparatus were visually read.
OCR had misidentified an introductory variant's7:15 label as7:13; the printed
page and local apparatus both show7:15. It cannot supply the missing13 evidence.
The original Cologne transcription, modern apparatus and decisive target pixels
remain unacquired. Earlier frozen contracts and their access history stay intact.
