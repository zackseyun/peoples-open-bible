# First Samuel 6 19 casualty count comparison

## Outcome

Retain POB's difficult number sequence provisionally. The inspected Hebrew and
selected Greek both include seventy men and fifty thousand men. Josephus's
seventy-person account is meaningful contrary evidence, but it is a retelling,
not a surviving Hebrew manuscript or proof of its exact wording. Numerical
plausibility alone does not justify deleting fifty thousand from the source.
Earliest wording remains unresolved; no canonical record changes in this pass.

## Source and version controls

WLC 6:19 has שבעים איש חמשים אלף איש, with both numerical phrases and no
intervening conjunction. Root and one read-only Hebrew analyst inspect actual
6:13–21, 4:10 and Numbers 1:46. The narrative response and relocation in
6:20–21 do not supply a casualty total. Numbers 1:46 explicitly coordinates a
compound census total, but that single contrast does not invalidate the unusual
Samuel syntax. “Seventy out of fifty thousand” supplies a relationship not
expressed by an explicit out-of preposition. An additive 50,070 interpretation
supplies conjunction/order normalization. Seventy alone deletes source content.

The actual [Rahlfs/Hanhart 2006 publisher text](https://www.die-bibel.de/bibel/LXX/1SA.6)
reads ἑβδομήκοντα ἄνδρας καὶ πεντήκοντα χιλιάδας ἀνδρῶν at 6:19. Its explicit
conjunction supports an additive interpretation. The opening instead concerns
the sons of Jeconiah not rejoicing at seeing the ark: this is not the same
offense wording as POB's “looked into.” Comparing the number alone does not
approve a hybrid opening or establish uniform Greek manuscript support.

Root reads [Josephus Antiquities 6.16 and its surrounding account](https://greekdoc.com/DOCUMENTS/josephus/ant-6.html),
including Greek and translations. The page identifies its Greek as Niese's
edition; no manuscript apparatus or edition scan is inspected here. It has
seventy villagers and attributes the offense to non-priests approaching the ark,
being unworthy to touch it. This can reflect a smaller source count or narrative
adaptation; this body check does not discriminate those histories or establish
independence from Greek tradition. Adjacent modern commentary on that page is
not adopted as ancient testimony. The Chicago presentation timed out; its
search excerpt is not evidence of a completed body consultation.

The actual [NET author note](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Samuel+6%3A19&version=NET)
reports a few medieval Hebrew copies lacking fifty thousand and favors the
larger count on external evidence. This is a modern editorial report and
argument, not new manuscript collation. This pass does not identify or date
those individual copies, count versional majorities, or verify that report from
a full apparatus. Modern translation agreement contributes no ancient votes.
