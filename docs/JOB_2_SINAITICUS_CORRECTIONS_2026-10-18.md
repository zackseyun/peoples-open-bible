# Job 2 in the original and corrected Sinaiticus transcription

## Judgment

The Codex Sinaiticus Project's transcription distinguishes **πλανωμενη** in the
original text from the corrector **ca**'s **πλανητιϲ** at Job 2:9. This makes the
lexical caution in our [earlier comparison](JOB_2_WIFE_SPEECH_COMPARISON_2026-10-18.md)
more precise: the participle and noun belong to different reported states of
one manuscript. Neither the correction nor the project's edition is another
independent witness. The longer speech is already present in the original
state reported by the project; it is not wholly a ca addition.

Retain POB's Hebrew and main English provisionally. This evidence strengthens
the warning against a uniform rare-word argument for authorship. It does not
decide whether the original Greek translator or an earlier Greek reviser
composed the longer scene, recover a Hebrew long form, or establish a canon
addition. No source, translation, note or reader code is changed.

## The identified manuscript and its reported corrections

The [official passage page](https://www.codexsinaiticus.org/en/manuscript.aspx?book=32&chapter=2&verse=9)
identifies quire 71, folio 3 recto, British Library folio 186, scribe A.
Job 2:9 occupies column 2, lines 25–47; 2:10 starts at line 48 and continues
on 71-3v, column 1, lines 1–7, library folio 186b. The official page image was
viewed at overview and enlarged target-column scales. Its correction popup
also explicitly distinguishes original **πλανωμενη** from ca **πλανητιϲ**.
The hand assignments below remain the project's editorial readings, not newly
certified palaeography or recovered erased letters.

The [downloaded transcription](https://codexsinaiticus.org/en/project/transcription_download.aspx)
is version 1.04, dated 25 March 2014 in its header. Its entire Job 2:9–10
markup was examined, not the entire manuscript transcription. These are all
six encoded correction units in those verses. Greek spelling below follows
the XML, without supplying accents or silently standardizing sigma.

| Location | Reported original state | Reported ca state | Importance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 71-3r, col 2, line 28 | χρονον ετι μεικρον | αναμενω χρονον ετι μεικρον | A finite waiting verb is added; do not attribute the selected edition's complete quotation grammar to every state |
| Same column, line 30 | αυτου | μου | The possessive in the deliverance clause changes |
| Same column, line 37 | No word at this correction unit | καθη | A sitting verb is added; the surrounding worms and outdoor scene already remain |
| Same column, line 39 | πλανωμενη | πλανητιϲ | Participle and noun must be separated in lexical tests |
| Same column, line 41 | No word at this correction unit | περιερχομενη · | A movement participle and punctuation are added after the house-to-house expression |
| 71-3v, col 1, line 4 | οιποιϲομεν | υποιϲομεν | The project reports an orthographic correction in Job's response |

The original-state omissions are local to these correction units, not omissions
of whole surrounding clauses. Neither verse contains an encoded `supplied` or
`gap` element. That describes this transcription, not a guarantee that every
physical letter is unproblematic. The final command remains the say-something
wording, with a contracted divine name, rather than an explicit Greek curse
verb. The prior historical edition and this project are compatible at the
participle-versus-noun level; their spelling and editorial conventions must
still be kept distinct.

## How the encoding controls the comparison

The project's [XML specification](https://codexsinaiticus.org/download/XMLspecs_sinaiticus.pdf),
especially pages 2–3, defines `rdg type="main-corr"` as the original reading and
`rdg type="corr" n="ca"` as the correction. The first displayed reading is only
a display default, not a universal original-state marker. Empty readings and
margin pointers also have defined meanings; flattening all text would combine
incompatible states or duplicate additions. Use explicit reading types and
preserve their location and corrector labels in future comparisons.

The [reproducibility packet](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/job2_sinaiticus.2026-10-18.v1.json)
pins the ZIP, XML, specification, page renders and frozen predecessor. Raw
project assets remain private; the project's noncommercial attribution and
share-alike terms are not treated as permission to vendor its transcription
into POB's distributable source corpus. This report records bounded observations
and original analysis, not a converted manuscript edition.

## Remaining discriminating evidence

The modern local Greek apparatus and its other witnesses remain useful for
testing the sequence of readings. Sinaiticus's correction by itself cannot
date the composition of the longer speech. Do not repeat this transcription
as a second independent source or infer Hebrew priority from its age.

A bounded three-route search did not obtain the competing original studies:
Heater's genuine publisher preview stops at printed page 3, before target
pages 35–36; the CSIC announcement timed out; Peeters exposed catalogue metadata,
not Fernández Marcos's chapter. The collected reprint at pages 113–127 remains
a bibliographic lead, not a consulted argument. Keep those positions indirect
until actual target text becomes available; do not repeat these access routes
without a new lead.

For presentation, keep the reading text clean and the detailed evidence behind
the existing Check Provenance control. The user's conditional concern about
inline clutter is not approval for a separate publisher or reader redesign.
