## The citation dependency

Both readers also inspect the complete [Field I opening preface
facsimile](https://archive.org/download/origenhexapla01unknuoft/origenhexapla01unknuoft_jp2.zip/origenhexapla01unknuoft_jp2%2Forigenhexapla01unknuoft_0011.jp2?ext=jpg),
image0011: 560,444 bytes, 1465 by1956 pixels, SHA256
`2069e1e37791879dbc9b4f8596274371bdcf31b9bedb3900a39eba905feaa7a3`.

Field describes Morinus's1587 Greek annotations and Nobilius's subsequent-year
Latin publication. He acknowledges the conventional credit to Nobilius for
work properly credited to Morin. Footnote1 reports that his examination of
Bodleian collations of Cod248, identified as Vat346, persuaded him that Morin's
Proverbs readings derived from that one codex.

This is Field's published dependency judgment. Neither the actual collations
nor the codex's target leaf or physical date is inspected here. The practical
consequence is to trace the reporting chain, not count Field, Fox, Morin and
Nobilius as four independent ancient copies. Cod248 and Vat346 are reported
identifiers for one object, not two votes. A direct Vatican catalogue request
does not yield an inspectable target here; no leaf or date is inferred.

## A concrete POB metadata correction target

Root reads the unchanged current Proverbs30:1 YAML, SHA256
`d33087ede5520a0c758df4491848c1070d53178d83f85d47ad2ff3b4505ec8ef`.
Its final-word lexical rationale and theological alternative associate
וְאֻכָל directly with “I am weary.” That does not distinguish the examined
alternate readings' word mapping: their repeated weariness clauses come from
redividing the earlier לאיתיאל as לאיתי אל. Their final word is separately
disputed between ability/success and exhaustion; the current main text instead
reads Ucal. Even if weary is intended as a loose exhaustion paraphrase, naming
the distinct final-word analysis would make the rationale more precise.

The correction target is the rationale's mapping, not automatic replacement
of Ucal or proof of either alternate's priority. A future metadata-only
candidate should distinguish those words and analyses explicitly, retain
received-name versus reconstructed-verbal status, and undergo its own review.
No such candidate is applied here. This root finding is separate from the
judge's two-leaf source-observation approval.
