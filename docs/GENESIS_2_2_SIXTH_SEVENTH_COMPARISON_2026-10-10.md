# Genesis 2:2: completion on the sixth or seventh day

Date: October 10, 2026. Baseline: `c6c50aeb017482417ed6b552ead839ed3580df7a`.
Status: bounded published-source comparison; canonical record unchanged.

## Result

Retain the declared Masoretic seventh-day completion provisionally. A local
working preference for seventh is defensible from the connected seventh-day
pattern and the possibility that sixth removes an apparent difficulty.
Historical priority remains unresolved: assimilation of an original sixth to
the repeated seventh is a live contrary explanation. Neither proposal is an
observed scribal motive.

This is a consequential known source difference, not newly deciphered Hebrew.
POB currently renders the retained seventh as “By the seventh day God had
finished.” That interpretive translation is a separate question from changing
the Hebrew numeral. A qualified sixth-day disclosure and a separate on/by
English comparison are worthwhile next contributions. No reader application,
fresh review certification or canon decision occurs in this packet.

### Hebrew controls and Samaritan provenance

WLC's two completion/cessation numerals are seventh. The actual connected
controls read were Genesis 1:31; 2:1–3; Exodus 20:8–11 and 31:17.
Genesis 1:31 closes the sixth day; 2:1 summarizes completion; 2:3 blesses the
seventh. Exodus describes six creation days and seventh-day cessation, but is
not an independent manuscript reading of Genesis 2:2.

The [pinned Samaritan dataset README](https://github.com/DT-UCPH/sp/blob/2f2120286ac48d4ff3d04e0107e33efd864aa9e1/README.md)
identifies Genesis's basis as the Samaritanus/Schorch transcription of Dublin
Chester Beatty Library 751. Garizim 1 supplies late Deuteronomy, not this target.
The existing repository extractor verifies the README plus seven feature
hashes and reads sign slots rather than confusing morphological nodes with
written words. Root independently reproduces completion `הששי` and cessation
`השביעי`, with surrounding Genesis 1:31; 2:1,3 read.

A material provenance limit emerged: the actual pinned
[textual-issues file](https://github.com/DT-UCPH/sp/blob/2f2120286ac48d4ff3d04e0107e33efd864aa9e1/textual_issues/issues.json)
contains 29 entries, including editorial harmonizations with MT or core SP
elsewhere. None identifies Genesis 2:2. Thus this is an edited digital control,
not an assertion that every sign is an unaltered diplomatic transcription of

### Dead Sea Scroll preservation and discovery denominator

The reacquired pinned QDR biblical index has 266 records. An exact word-tag
query `Gen 2:2` returns 4Q10 fragment 4 physical lines 1–3 only. This is an
index-query result, not a census of all discovered witnesses or proof that no
other manuscript preserves the verse. Nearby exact tags 2:1 and 2:3 provide
line context.

The actual [published 4Q10 transcription](https://forum.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q10/2026-05-21/index.html#c279386-i279393)
was read across fragment 4 lines 1–4. Line 2 begins a supplied span containing
the completion numeral and ends with another supplied span containing the
cessation numeral. Some intervening work/relative-clause ink is marked
uncertain; it does not rescue either numeral as preserved. The
[edition's FAQ](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/faq/v1/en/index.html)
identifies brackets as restoration and circelli as uncertainty, and warns
against treating unchecked working variants as manuscripts. The displayed
seventh numerals cannot be counted as extant 4Q10 support for seventh.
No original pixels, fresh letter reading or physical dating were obtained.

## Competing explanations and chronological sensitivity

For seventh: the repeated seventh-day completion, cessation and blessing can
form a connected literary pattern. Sixth may clarify completion before rest
and align with the preceding sixth-day close and six-day creation controls.

For sixth: the early Greek textual tradition's different numeral and the
Samaritan Hebrew control make it a serious source alternative. Seventh may
arise by assimilation to the immediately following cessation day or verse 3.
Repeated wording can favor either literary design or copying assimilation;
it is not a one-way proof.

The target is a written Hebrew numeral difference, not merely pointing.
No single-letter numerical-abbreviation confusion was verified. We have not
established a physical-manuscript chronology or independence sufficient to
rank the two Hebrew forms. A modest preference for earlier attestation must
not convert supplied 4Q10 into a vote or date editions as ancient witnesses.
Removing such a chronological preference still leaves priority unresolved.

## English question kept separate

The current [Genesis 2:2 record](../translation/ot/genesis/002/002.yaml) has
one interpretive note and historical agreement metadata. Its rationale calls
“had finished” best and explicitly harmonizes the day phrase with rest.
Those are existing claims, not fresh validation. “By the seventh day” can
also describe a deadline including that day, rather than necessarily before
it begins. Neither seventh nor “on” necessarily asserts additional creative
activity throughout that day.

The actual [GKC §111](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Gesenius%27_Hebrew_Grammar/111._The_Imperfect_with_W%C4%81w_Consecutive)
and [§119h](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Gesenius%27_Hebrew_Grammar/119._The_Subordination_of_Nouns_to_the_Verb_by_means_of_Prepositions)
provide general sequencing/contextual tense and temporal-preposition controls.
§111q's specific pluperfect setup depends on a preceding pluperfect perfect;
Genesis 2:1 itself has a wayyiqtol summary, not that setup. This does not
prove or exclude every contextual anterior reading here. Temporal ב alone
does not establish an exclusive English deadline. The relative “that he had
done” does not force the main completion verb into the English pluperfect.

A subsequent frozen comparison should test “on … finished” against
“by … had finished,” retaining seventh in both and making the strong contrary
case explicit. Then review the entire draft, note anchors, lexical rationale,
historical-review archives and actual full-Genesis export. Disclose sixth as
a source alternative without claiming an independently inspected Syriac
edition or secure DSS numeral support. Do not transfer old agreement scores.
