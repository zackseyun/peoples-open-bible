# 1 Enoch 6–17 missing-verse draft recovery

After repairing the Charles 1906 cross-page Geʿez parser, the POB inventory
for chapters 6–17 exposed 42 absent verse files (chapter 11 was already
complete). This branch contains **42 new English drafts** for those exact
verse units, made with the existing structured POB Enoch drafting workflow.
Every draft's `source.text` matches the recovered primary Geʿez row byte for
byte and its status remains `draft`.

This is **not a reviewed translation or a deployment**. Eleven existing
last-verse records in chapters 6–17 still need source/English correction,
and the already-published 5:9 is also clipped. No new Graphic Reader chapter
may be treated as complete until those older verses have been repaired,
the new drafts have independent editorial review, POB exports are verified,
and any newly needed art and verse mapping have been reviewed and published.
The `codex/enoch-source-gaps` branch is the review surface; do not merge these
draft YAMLs to `main` merely because their hashes and verse numbers pass.
