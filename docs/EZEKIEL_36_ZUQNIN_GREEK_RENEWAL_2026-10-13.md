# Ezekiel 36 renewal promise in Zuqnin Greek undertext

Tisserant's edition of **ZVI** supplies an actually read Greek witness to the
new-heart/new-spirit promise and divine agency at Ezekiel 36:26–27. This is a
newly verified published reading for POB, not a newly discovered manuscript or
fresh decipherment. It corroborates part of the longer Greek passage without
settling which literary form came first. Retain the declared Hebrew and current
English; keep historical priority on HOLD.

## Source identity and editorial conventions

The [custodial entry](https://digi.vatlib.it/mss/detail/264993) identifies
Vat.sir.162, Zvi undertext, assigns 501–600, and cites Tisserant's edition.
The Greek layer's date is distinct from the later Syriac writing over it.
Tisserant's printed lxxxi–lxxxv identifies this Ezekiel/Daniel group separately
from **ZV**, discussed immediately beforehand. His palaeographic discussion
excludes a date before the sixth century; neither this review nor the catalogue
is independent physical dating.

The [Robarts scan](https://archive.org/details/codexzuqninensis00tissuoft)
reproduces Eugène Tisserant, *Codex Zuqninensis rescriptus Veteris Testamenti*,
Studi e testi 23, Roma 1911. The scanned copy's verso bears a **1977 anastatic
reprint** imprint. Record edition year, reprint year and manuscript date
separately. The 404-page, 16,750,587-byte PDF remains outside Git at
`/tmp/tisserant-zuqnin-GDFaOK/codexzuqninensis1911.pdf`, SHA256
`1093cc5ebbd79115b4ceb5a2d8dcbecf80c4015ebb8f86fbbe02107c7614794c`.

The complete critical-sign key at PDF 101 distinguishes doubtful underdotted

## Actual renewal passage coverage

Printed 253–254 / PDF 355–356 contain 36:20–28. At printed 254:

| Locus | Published preservation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 36:26, column I 18–23, II 1–7 | New-heart/new-spirit wording with several supplied word beginnings; the spirit abbreviation itself is unbracketed. Stone/flesh-heart wording continues unbracketed in II. | A positive partial witness, not every letter of a complete reconstructed verse. |
| 36:27, II 7–17 | Unbracketed spirit abbreviation with my, giving within you, divine action, walking, keeping and doing; explicit final αὐτά. | Published Greek supports the renewal/agency comparison. It does not recover independent Hebrew letters or original Hebrew pointing. |
| 36:28, II 18–23 | Land given to the fathers and the start of the people clause; the page ends within the word for people. | The remaining people wording and the divine-identity clause are not readable coverage on this leaf. |

The whole 36:27 expression is outside lacuna brackets, doubtful-letter marks
and editorial restitution signs in this printed reading. No correction or
target apparatus note is attached on this page. Sacred-name expansion remains
an interpretation of the actual abbreviation, not additional preserved letters.
This is Tisserant's reading of Greek undertext, not our pixel autopsy.

The edition header calls printed 254 **Fol.78v**, but the final concordance at
printed 272 / PDF 374 maps it to **current bound-codex 78r**. Printed 253 maps
to current 78v. Keep both locator systems; do not open a modern scan using an
unchecked edition-header face. Missing leaves between the surviving chapter-36

## Source and English judgment

Current POB 36:26–28 retains new heart, new spirit, my Spirit within you,
divine enabling, statutes/judgments and the land promise. This Greek witness
adds inspected source evidence for those compared expressions, not an English
replacement. Its explicit αὐτά at 36:27 also gives a concrete published witness
for the [earlier group-level addition report](EZEKIEL_36_LANGUAGE_CONTROLS_2026-10-12.md#reading-level-affiliation-in-ezekiel).
The pronoun cannot decide inherited Hebrew-ending misanalysis against ordinary
Greek object clarification.

Tisserant places ZVI's Ezekiel in the Lucianic recension and compares it with ZV
at lxxxiii–lxxxiv. That is his affiliation assessment, not an independent Hebrew
vote. His own discussion distinguishes their local omissions and variants.
In his key, α denotes five specified manuscripts; do not silently identify it
with another edition's group notation. Neither agreement, age nor fuller
wording by itself establishes the ancestor. The [Masada comparison](EZEKIEL_36_LONGER_FORM_EVIDENCE_2026-10-12.md)
remains direct-language Hebrew evidence with its own preservation limits.
