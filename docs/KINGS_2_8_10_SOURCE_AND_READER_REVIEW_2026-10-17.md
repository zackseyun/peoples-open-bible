Date: 2026-10-17. Method: v2.0.15. Historical baseline: `12fa8aad57a9bb573177c4a5864c0e9ee62b369d`.

## Outcome and scope

Retain POB's positive recovery message provisionally. Correct its explanation and disclose the negative written alternative. The vendored Hebrew edition explicitly records ketiv לא (“not”) and qere לו (“to him”); the positive message is therefore not merely a modern conjecture prompted by an unintelligible text. This is an attested reading-tradition distinction, not a newly recovered Hebrew letter.

The present rationale incorrectly says the negative reading conflicts with the following death announcement. It agrees with that announcement. The more consequential narrative difference is how Hazael's positive report in verse 14 relates to Elisha's instruction. Neither a desire for narrative smoothness nor a preferred account of prophetic truthfulness decides historical priority.

The proposed application changes the note, moves its anchor to the positive recovery quotation, and repairs only the associated lexical and theological explanations. The Hebrew source object and marker-free main English remain unchanged. Historical source priority is **HOLD**. This does not certify the whole verse, neighboring verses, an earliest Greek reconstruction, or the entire Hebrew corpus. It supplies no argument for changing the biblical canon.
