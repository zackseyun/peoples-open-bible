English 2:8) does not choose between “from her days” and “her waters.”

analysis and a scholarly counterargument to the water interpretation.

## Directly consulted grammar and argument

[Davidson, Hebrew Syntax (1902), printed p.1, §1 Remark 1](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Introductory_Hebrew_Grammar-_Hebrew_Syntax_(1902).djvu/18)
discusses the Masoretic phrase as an elliptical relative temporal construction.
He supplies the regular temporal form מִיָּמֶיהָ, whose consonants are מימיה,
and notes textual doubt. Root and the grammar subtask inspect the actual scan,
not only its unproofread OCR. This is a grammatical proposal, not preserved
Qumran vowels or proof of original syntax.

Root also checks these actual local WLC/OSHB controls: 1 Samuel 25:28 and
Job 38:12 have temporal מִיָּמֶיךָ forms; Jeremiah 6:7 and 50:38 have water
מֵימֶיהָ forms. They establish real temporal and water constructions, not an
exact feminine temporal occurrence or a statistically discriminating rule.

[Gesenius–Kautzsch–Cowley §135d–f](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Gesenius%27_Hebrew_Grammar/135),
actually read, distinguishes a separate pronoun emphasizing an existing suffix.
It does not independently prove the exact Nahum construction. Following היא
is supplied in the consulted 4Q82 row; a possible suffix-plus-pronoun analysis
must not be represented as surviving complete syntax.

[Lange, “4QXIIg (4Q82) as an Editorial Text,” Textus 26 (2016), p.10](https://bibleproject.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/he_bible_project/files/a._lange.pdf)
accepts the additional-he spelling, rejects the Greek water interpretation,
and proposes loss of final ה before following היא by haplography. That is
his argument, not an established Greek mistake. His cited De Rossi 309 and
Vulgate readings were not independently collated here. His p.5 n.11 says DSS
quotations follow Biblia Qumranica 3b; citing DJD XV is not consulting its
target apparatus. Root reads the title and relevant discussion and visually
checks p.10. The final ה in its first target listing has a visible circellus;
the siglum's exact conventions were not verified. Do not equate that displayed
qualification with the unmarked 2026 Qumran Digital transcription or claim
the later publication proves a corrected letter reading.

ἦν/τείχη alternatives in the preceding expression, but no target days-versus-

hypothesis must not be read as the Greek's unambiguous grammar. We do not
publish a hybrid whole-verse water translation on that basis.
