POB provisionally retains SBLGNT's all the ungodly. Every soul is a substantive
Greek alternative, not another meaning of the same words. The comparison

retained wording has defeated its alternative historically. No Greek or
marker-free main English change is proposed. The

## Transmission arguments

Root inspected all eight native pages of Gerd Mink's discussion, printed
181–188, PDF 189–196, in the [SBL-hosted volume](https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Wachtel-Holmes.pdf).
Its apparatus assigns every soul to P72, 01 and 1852, and all the ungodly to
03 and others; their is a separate addition. Mink argues that the three
every-soul witnesses are too dissimilar for a close shared transmission
explanation, making that wording a strong initial-text candidate. Their
agreement percentages are relationship measures, not source probabilities.
His footnote 78 nevertheless allows Romans 2:9 to influence three independent
changes. Footnote 79 documents substantial local contamination and neighboring
omissions. The coherence case is therefore useful but not logically conclusive.

[Flink, pages 116–118](https://www.bsw.org/filologia-neotestamentaria/vol-20-2007/reconsidering-the-text-of-jude-5-13-15-and-18/224/article-p116.html),
favors all the ungodly on distribution and style while acknowledging competing
internal explanations. Harmonization toward Enoch or Jude 4 can explain one
direction; Romans 2:9 influence or repetition avoidance can explain the other.
Enoch's all flesh is not itself an attestation of Jude's every soul. Broad
distribution, manuscript age, or repeated editions cannot alone choose the text.

## Decision and English consequence

Retain the declared SBLGNT base provisionally and expose every soul at the
object phrase. This is a base-retention decision under unresolved priority,
not proof that all the ungodly is earliest. The stronger competing candidate
is supported by actual early-witness readings and a reasoned published coherence
argument; its plausible alternative derivation prevents automatic promotion.
The retained wording also has an ancient direct Greek witness and a defensible
published countercase. Neither side is dismissed because of its theology.

Every soul can be rendered every person. It changes the explicit conviction
object, while the preceding judgment clause already says against all.
Neither variant alone proves universal salvation or a changed canon.
The candidate moves existing note a to convict, b to him and c to harsh things,
and adds d at all the ungodly. Existing note wording, source and marker-free
English are preserved. No new lexical-book consultation, blinded English
comparison or whole-verse translation approval is claimed.

Reopen for discriminating hand/version evidence or a materially stronger
passage-specific transmission explanation, not another edition count or model
vote. Full OT/NT comparison and fresh damaged-ink calibration remain unfinished.
ImageGen supplied no evidence. Reader disclosure is a real but limited POB
contribution, not a novel ancient reading.
