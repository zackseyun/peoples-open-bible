# 1 Enoch 26:4 - targeted cross-page follow-up HOLD

Two further independent read-only reviews used Charles1906 printed64/PDF102,
printed66/PDF104, the facing apparatus page and an 800DPI detail crop. They
confirm that canonical 26:4 stops before a cross-page continuation ending at
ሠለስቲሆሙ። immediately before numbered 5. The parser miscopies the second-
valley word (reviewers read ወካልእት), and the selected text has an asterisk
before the first ቈላ. Only the second valley is expressly deep and dry;
ጽንፈ is singular. The final 'three' refers contextually to the mountains,
not a verified modern site.

Both targeted passes HOLD exact source and English approval because the
height/extent token's final fidel and vowel cannot be securely established
from these images. 'Not high' is provisional. The facing Greek does not settle
the Ethiopic. Dillmann 1851 chapter OCR has a different form, ሩኅ, but its
original scan has not been independently aligned or checked for this unit and
cannot be silently substituted for Charles. Better primary evidence or
qualified source adjudication is needed before a complete replacement verse.
