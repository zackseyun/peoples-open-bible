# 1 Enoch 25: seven-unit scan review, source HOLD

Two independent read-only Codex passes checked all seven selected Charles1906
printed62-64/PDF100-102 units, including the facing Greek/apparatus page and
400DPI primary images in the second pass. Both find the current chapter
incomplete: POB 25:5 stops at the page break after ዲበ; 25:6-7 are absent
although they appear before the XXVI header in the OCR file named ch26.txt.

The second pass requests revisions for 25:1-4 and 25:7 and holds 25:5-6.
The fruit/life and north-direction wording of 25:5 is critically marked,
and independent passes disagree on the exact life-word vowel: it cannot be
certified from this packet. In 25:6 the small raised 7 is an apparatus
reference, not a license to translate “seven bones”; the adjacent verb and
fragrance/bones phrase remain unresolved. Greek alternatives and Charles's
conjectures cannot silently replace the printed Ethiopic. No species, modern
location, invented continuation, or unmarked resolution was installed.

No canonical YAML, graphic artwork, catalog, app UI or full-book publication
was approved by this package. Fresh scan-bound source repair, translated notes
and a whole-chapter review are still needed before source application. The
attached input/result/provenance files and image hashes preserve the evidence;
scan bitmaps remain outside Git.
