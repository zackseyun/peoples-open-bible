## Decisive diplomatic readings

2. **Stand verb: ይቀውሙ, not ይቀውም.** The final body glyph has the right-side appendage of second-order ሙ, not the descending-left-stem sixth-order ም. It is visibly the same type of ending as the last ሙ of the source's ምስሎሙ on the next native page. The actual1907 TableI, row4 Māi, shows II With ū as ሙ and VI With ĕ or without any vowel as ም. The table's header and entire row were actually inspected. An earlier modern Kefa Unicode orientation image was generated as a non-primary orientation aid only; it is NOT the proof. TableI and the actual source are the proof. Actual1907 p204 §101 explains that plural masculine ū is appended to the imperfect's third-person prefix. Thus this selected predicate is third-person masculine plural, not singular. The subsequent `ወይከውን` and `ወኢይክል` remain singular; the source is not to be harmonized.
3. **Penultimate: ኀዲገ.** The actual native token has first ኀ, middle ዲ, final first-order ገ. It is not the apparatus-normal `ኀዲግ` with sixth-order final ግ. The selected printed final ገ is retained. Actual1907 p265 §124 prints the base `ኀዲግ` in its stemI,1 formation discussion, expressly glossed `to abandon`; it describes long accented ī after the second-last radical. Actual1907 p268 §125 says that in the Simple Ground-Stem this inner form also serves the nominal-infinitive use. Most decisively, actual1907 p453 §182(a)(α), in its discussion of the accusative substantive-infinitive after ability verbs, prints `ኢይክል፡ ኀዲገ፡ አቡሁ` with the example's explanation `he cannot leave his father` (Gen44,22). This is a primary grammatical example, not a familiar modern Bible translation used to replace the selected text. It controls the precise selected inflection: accusative infinitive in -a, hence final first-order ገ. The selected negative ability + ኀዲገ construction is therefore not an unresolved malformed infinitive, and need not be normalized to the apparatus's form.
4. **Final noun: ምስሎሙ.** Actual native leaf123 has four body fidels, not five `ምስሌሆሙ`. Actual1865 col173 nominal ምስል has `1) similitudo, forma, figura; 2) parabola, proverbium, simile; 3) effigies, imago, simulacrum, statua`; the entry's concluding note discusses singular noun/plural-fashion suffixing and distinguishes the later preposition. Actual preposition entry starts174 and continues175 with suffix shapes and `instar, sicut`, then primary `cum`. The source's four-fidel noun token must not be conflated with that preposition. Actual1907 p354–355 §154(b)(α) explains singular consonant-final nominal accusative suffixing and the contracted third-person plural -omu forms (rather than -ahomu). That controls `their form` as a nominal object; it does not identify an otherwise unstated antecedent or authorize adding `new`.

## Consequence for exact current0394 packet

WHOLE HOLD, not acceptance with caveats. The current source misprints ከመ for actual እፎ and ይቀውም for actual ይቀውሙ. Its source note and mandatory note b positively assert that all predicates are singular, and the English `it rises` implements that wrong assertion. They must change. Penultimate ኀዲገ and nominal ምስሎሙ are supported, not reasons to retain the wrong stand predicate. Retaining only the old English with a caveat would still falsify the selected source.

A separate new corrective author proposal may preserve plural `they arise`, then singular `it becomes`/`cannot`, without replacing the plural subject by explicit `the stars`. That proposal is not the input0394 and cannot inherit its old author's vote or be published on this record alone. The relation remains compressed; ordinary English pronouns expose its number shift, not resolve its antecedents.
